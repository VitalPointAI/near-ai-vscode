# Doctrine terms register

The status of every doctrine term the staff templates use where the source was in question. The values live in `src/domain/staff/doctrine.ts`, one copy per term with a source note beside it; this register says how far each is confirmed. Sources are cited by title and edition only. No text from a restricted publication is quoted here or in the code.

Status key:

- **Confirmed**: read in a public source; the edition is named.
- **Confirmed by owner**: the owner checked it against their own references, or decided it.
- **Best guess**: inferred from the nearest public source; the row says what would confirm it.

Updated 19 Nov 2026. Rows follow the numbering of the doctrine terms research (items 1a to 23, with 11b and 12b), plus the two owner confirmations at the end.

## How to change a term

Change the value in `src/domain/staff/doctrine.ts` (its tests name the expected value), update its source note there, and update its row here. A best guess becomes confirmed only when a person reads the named source, and the row then names that source and edition.
