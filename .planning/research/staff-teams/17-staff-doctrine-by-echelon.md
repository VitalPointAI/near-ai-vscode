# US, NATO and Canadian staff requirements by echelon

## Baselines for SovAI

Use US Army, NATO and NATO-aligned Canadian profiles for the same institutional staff
catalogue. Echelon, component, organization, contributing nation and appointing authority
are independent fields. In this profile, **unit** means a battalion, regiment or equivalent
with a staffed headquarters. A company or detachment with fewer positions inherits only the
duties actually assigned to it. The comparison covers how to prepare, review and exchange
administrative staff products. A source that describes a broader duty supports only the
institutional part of the duty here.

| Profile | Doctrine and staff basis | Requirements consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US Army national land staff | The Army's FM 6-0 distinguishes coordinating, special, and personal staff. The executive officer or chief of staff integrates staff work. S- and G-designators belong to the actual headquarters and establishment. US joint JP 5-0 is a separate reference for joint headquarters. | Keep an Army land staff template separate from a US joint staff template. Map the actual S/G office or supporting specialist. Preserve independent finance, legal, records and personnel authority. Do not generate a missing staff section from a catalogue number. [Army FM 6-0](#primary-sources) · [US Joint Chiefs of Staff planning series](https://www.jcs.mil/Doctrine/Joint-Doctrine-Pubs/5-0-Planning-Series/) |
| NATO multinational land and joint staff | AJP-01 and AJP-3 describe the Allied joint framework and a tailorable joint staff. AJP-3.2 places land headquarters in that framework. AJP-5 is the Allied joint planning baseline. APP-28 is the Allied tactical land planning reference. Neither a national formation nor a multinational land headquarters automatically becomes a joint headquarters. | Configure a multinational land headquarters from its approved charter, reporting relationships and assigned functions. Configure a joint headquarters separately where established. Keep the NATO requirement, national contribution, host-nation channel and source-owner release authority distinct. [AJP-01](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ajp-01-d-allied-joint-doctrine) · [AJP-3](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/allied-joint-doctrine-for-the-conduct-of-operations-ajp-3b) · [AJP-3.2](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/allied-joint-doctrine-for-land-operations-ajp-32) |
| Canada within the NATO-aligned baseline | Owner decision for 2026–27 requirements. Use the NATO family, with APP-28 Edition B Version 1 for the tactical land staff baseline and AJP-5 for an explicitly assigned operational joint staff function. The Canadian Army publicly described its APP-28 transition and Canadian Forces College's intended AJP-5 teaching transition in 2026–27. | Canadian unit, brigade and division profiles inherit the NATO-aligned process vocabulary and add Canadian appointment, approval and records requirements. Legacy CAOPP/CFOPP terms may be mapped when reading approved historical material. Confirm actual establishment, approved editions and local implementation before live use. [Canadian Army transition notice](https://www.canada.ca/en/army/services/line-sight/articles/2026/04/operational-and-tactical-planning-in-the-caf.html) · [AJP-5 Edition B Version 1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/allied-joint-publication-ajp-05a-allied-joint-doctrine-for-the-planning-of-operations) |

The CAF term *pan-domain* and US Army *multidomain operations* belong in the same search and
profile navigation family. NATO also uses *Multi-Domain Operations*. The overlap supports a
shared requirement for accountable information exchange across components, domains and
partner organizations. Each uses different doctrine and authorities. Joint describes participation across armed services or components. Establishing a joint
headquarters requires its own charter. Multinational describes participation by different
nations. A land headquarters can support both and retain its land mandate. Keep these qualifiers independent of physical echelon. The
US, Allied and Canadian source terms remain visible in imported records and user-facing
profile labels. [CAF joint training definitions](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/policies-standards/defence-administrative-orders-directives/8000-series/8015/8015-0-canadian-armed-forces-joint-training.html) · [NATO ACT concept](https://www.act.nato.int/activities/multi-domain-operations/) · [Canadian PDC2 concept](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/reports-publications/pan-domain-command-control.html)

## Headquarters and national requirements

The staff forms are examples and design defaults. The attached authority record controls
which organizational position actually receives, reviews or signs each product.

| Echelon | US Army | NATO multinational land staff | Canadian national staff on NATO baseline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unit | The battalion executive officer integrates assigned staff work. Use the actual S-section or assigned supporting office. The unit owns source-level confirmations, exceptions and corrections. Output is a verified unit record and the minimum requested roll-up. A locally absent specialist receives a referral through the supporting unit or service. | A contributing unit retains its own source and national administrative chain. A multinational unit gives an authorized parent headquarters agreed fields under the contributing-nation and host arrangements. Output is a national submission, approved extract and receiving receipt where required. Establish which nation or authority owns each contribution. | Use the commanding officer, adjutant, orderly room or other assigned function and supporting Canadian service offices. Match the unit's approved NATO-aligned formats for its role. Preserve the Canadian personnel or records authority, approved language requirements, and release direction in the national record. |
| Brigade | The brigade integrates unit submissions, reconciles duplicates within scope, owns its coordination register and resolves matters delegated to it. The approved establishment and appointments determine actual S/G structure and supporting specialists. Route portfolio, finance and professional issues to the authorized provider. | The multinational brigade defines what a complete submission looks like, verifies contributors' release conditions and reconciles differences in national formats. Share approved aggregates and exceptions with the designated division; retain custody with the originator where required. Keep national and NATO receipts distinct. | Assign the actual CMBG offices and supporting services. A Canadian brigade on a national chain can combine domestic returns for a delegated Canadian decision. A Canadian-led NATO brigade uses the NATO profile for the NATO product and the Canada overlay for national authority and restrictions. The same person serving in both roles acts under the recorded appointment for that decision. |
| Division | The division G-staff sets common definitions and validates brigade returns. Its staff coordinates the division-wide decision package, follows up on recurring discrepancies and refers policy or funding issues to the authority above division. Use assigned G-sections, professional advisers and servicing offices. The division headquarters battalion S-1 supports its own personnel separately from the division G-1. | A multinational land division sets common reporting and receipt conditions for its formations under the charter, reconciles exceptions across national and brigade boundaries and prepares aggregate evidence for the authorized higher headquarters. National disclosure and custody conditions travel with each contribution. | Use the national division mandate and assigned staff offices. A Canadian Army division is a national formation; Canadian participation in a NATO multinational division is a separate context. Keep national division and NATO headquarters approval, custody, reference sets and recipient lists separately addressable. A joint appointment uses the AJP-5 profile only for its assigned operational joint planning function. |

The Canadian Army's public examples establish that a unit can have an adjutant and orderly
room and that Canadian brigade staff positions can use G designators. They do not establish
the full current establishment across formations. The September 2026 Army reorganization
also changed the relationship between some brigades and divisions. For an example of separate
national and NATO chains, Canada's published arrangements place its NATO Multinational
Brigade Latvia in the Multinational Division North chain while Canadian authorities remain
separately identifiable. ## Duty families and expected products by echelon

Read each catalogue duty through its own source basis, the headquarters row above, and the
responsible authority below. The allocation and artifacts below are proposed SovAI
requirements. The three duty inventories give the actions, individual outputs, permissions and
skills. Where a branch has no local owner, mark its duty **supported by** a named external
office, **held pending assignment**, or **outside local mandate**. Keep both the producing
owner and decision authority visible.

| Duty family | Unit prepares and verifies | Brigade reconciles and coordinates | Division assures and sets conditions | Profile-specific responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CG · Command group | Decision intake, unit calendar, authorized minutes, named actions and overdue exceptions. | Consolidated issues, agenda and exact-version decision docket. Formal disposition under delegated authority. | Cross-formation decisions, exception register and assurance follow-up. | US XO or COS; NATO HQ chair and charter; CAF commanding officer, COS or delegated board. One official record per forum. |
| J1 · Personnel | Validate case evidence in the authorized system and release only permitted counts, referrals or corrections. | Reconcile periods and capacity returns. Identify missing submissions without expanding access to individual cases. | Maintain definitions, comparisons, trends and referrals to personnel policy authority. | US S/G-1; NATO staff checks national definitions and eligibility boundaries; CAF office retains Canadian case custody and recourse. |
| J2-IS · Information stewardship | Cite permitted sources, verify version and correct local evidence. | Assemble scoped provenance, check contradictory sources and reconcile vocabulary without exposing hidden items. | Maintain approved vocabulary and data-quality decisions. Report recurring issues to data owners. | S/G-2, NATO J2 and Canadian offices can have wider mandates. Appoint a separate data steward wherever the actual establishment gives that role to another office. |
| J3 · Staff coordination | Maintain local office handover, forum input and closure evidence. | Operate branch calendars, action registers and versioned forum records. | Resolve cross-formation administrative dependencies and assign a single authoritative forum and receiving owner. | US S/G-3 or command secretariat. NATO/CAF use the locally authorized action owner; no implied right to direct another component or nation. |
| J4 · Ordinary services and assurance | Reconcile ordinary office requisitions, authorized service tickets, inventory and receiving evidence. | Consolidate office demand, unresolved receiving exceptions and service dependencies. | Set reporting definitions for ordinary business services and review long-running exceptions. | US S/G-4 or servicing office; NATO coordination leaves national supplier and contractual decisions with the authority; CAF follows delegated procurement and stock custody. |
| J5 · Institutional planning | Describe proposed internal change, local constraints and source evidence. | Assemble feasibility and alternatives for business change, consolidate dependencies and assign sponsorship. | Prepare integrated administrative business case and evaluation record; route policy and funding decisions to delegates. | US G-5 or assigned programme office, and no assumed S-5. NATO/CAF profile references versioned doctrine vocabulary for interoperability while using the appropriate corporate approval process for administrative work. |
| J6-IT · Enterprise IT | Record user-facing service issues, asset evidence and controlled referral. | Consolidate defensive service evidence, interface exceptions and unresolved integrity or recovery items. | Coordinate shared service and data standards, attestations and authorized recovery evidence. | US S/G-6 or service provider; NATO and CAF retain separate identity, device and service scopes by organization. |

## Cross-echelon and cross-organization handoff

Capture command or reporting relationship, service support, administrative delegation,
records custody and disclosure as separate fields. In the NATO profile, national
administrative authority stays with the nation unless a separate delegation covers the
matter. An assignment to a multinational headquarters or to a framework nation does not
confer personnel or financial authority, custody or disclosure rights. Record the verified
delegate, subject, scope, effective dates and approving source. In Canada, apply the
Canadian national delegation to Canadian records and decisions. Temporary appointments
require their own current authority for the exact effect. [AJP-01, pp. 119–120](https://coi.nato.int/EWCOI/EW%20COI%20Shared%20Documents/EMO%20DOCTRINE%20PREP/AJP%20HARMONIZATION/AJP-01%20EDF%20V1%20E.pdf) · [Canadian QR&O, vol. I, ch. 4, arts. 4.20–4.21 and 4.26–4.27](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/policies-standards/queens-regulations-orders/vol-1-administration/ch-4-duties-responsibilities-officers.html)

A receiving staff office publishes its authorized request, shared vocabulary, due window and
required fields. A producing office returns an approved product ID, version hash, explicit
coverage and exclusions, source authority, authorized audience, and any permitted exception
metadata. Roll up only approved values. Distinguish zero, missing, suppressed, and inapplicable
data. Review each new roll-up for inferences from counts, suppression and metadata. Apply
the [common release workflow](12-JOINT-STAFF-SUPPORT-BLUEPRINT.md), including separate
originator or source permission where required, the exact recipient, destination and channel,
and a current authority check at delivery. Tie receiving acceptance to the exact product, hash and recipient. An authorized correction
points to affected roll-ups and keeps open acknowledgments visible within scope. A parent
headquarters receives enough to act on the approved product. A narrower source grant stays
enforceable at every echelon.

Provision the mapped agents for read-scoped source and document retrieval, draft writing,
authorized aggregate calculations, terminology mapping, provenance, exact-version review
packets and correction tracking. Human owners approve sources, define fields and schedules,
rule on policy and jurisdiction, sign dispositions, and authorize release. A dual-role user
selects their recorded capacity; the system still checks the authority and recipient. Teaching
or adopting a doctrine profile has no direct effect on access.

## Profile acceptance before live provisioning

| Case | Expected result with fictional records |
| --- | --- |
| Unit with no S-5, J7 or equivalent assigned | The branch lookup routes to its named support office, or leaves the duty visibly unassigned. An agent receives no inferred privileges. |
| US unit, brigade and division use different field versions | A roll-up shows missing and conflicting submissions and its version. Receipt and approval stay linked to the exact record the delegate reviewed. |
| One person holds separate Canadian and NATO appointments | Opening the national record uses Canadian scope. Preparing the Allied extract uses an independent audience decision and cannot see restricted evidence through metadata. |
| Canadian brigade changes from a legacy reference during 2026–27 | Historical records retain their edition and original wording. New profiles use the approved APP-28 baseline. Only the applicable joint workflow uses the approved AJP-5 edition. Any draft mapping awaits accountable verification. |
| Multinational division receives a correction for one originating unit | The correction preserves national custody and release marks, links permitted changed aggregates, and stays open pending acknowledgments from actual affected recipients. |
| Previously approved recipient loses access before a correction is delivered | Hold the correction and recheck authority. A cleared viewer or dual-role chair cannot give consent on the national originator’s behalf without its specific delegation. |
| National caveat and host channel disagree | Hold the affected handoff. The national authority and designated receiving office resolve the conflict with an authorized, exact-version scope record. |
