# Staff coordination, institutional planning, education and lessons

| Function | Scope of this catalogue | Human authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J3 staff coordination | Administrative forums, decision records, action tracking, office continuity and handover | Chief of Staff (COS) for staff-wide coordination. The meeting chair validates its record. The official holding delegated authority signs decisions. |
| J5 institutional planning | Corporate policy and administrative programmes, from intake through closeout | The sponsor owns scope and benefits. The appointed programme or policy lead owns the integrated draft. The applicable board or official decides within its delegation. |
| J7 education and lessons | Administrative learning, accessibility, approved qualifications, professional practice, and lessons management | The designated education authority approves objectives and curriculum. Assessors determine competence. The responsible authority validates corrective action and controls release. |

This is a product-design catalogue for administrative and institutional work. It covers office
continuity, records, governance, professional education, and the conduct of approved exercises
in those subjects. Every role name maps to a locally appointed position before configuration.
The workstream, data purpose, and exact action must remain in that approved scope. Requests for
live operational coordination, campaign or force employment planning, targeting, combat
instruction, or offensive cyber activity fall outside this catalogue.

## Evidence and applicability

**D** identifies a function with a direct source basis at the level named. Each row still proposes
a concrete artifact, human gate and agent capability. **I** identifies an inferred supporting
task or an administrative adaptation. **D/I** marks a row that combines a sourced function
with a proposed supporting step. The suggested request triggers, review intervals and
record fields are design defaults. The organization determines its actual cadence and system
of record. Local doctrine is cited by identifier and section and described in original wording.

Historical staff mappings may place these functions in a different directorate or consolidate
them in a smaller office. The placement of a function in the system never grants decision,
publication, assessment, personnel, financial, or records authority.

## J3 administrative coordination

Assign a human staff-coordination lead for administrative queue ownership. The COS or authorized
deputy owns staff-wide meeting priorities and resolves competing departmental demands. Secretaries
and recorders prepare the record for the chair. Working-group agreement remains a recommendation
until an appropriately delegated official decides. The records officer owns retention and holds.

| J3-01 **D** (S15-01/02) | New or recurring administrative forum. Use the approved staff priorities and delegated authority register. | Draft a forum charter identifying its purpose, chair, participants, frequency, input providers, outputs, and which later forum uses them. Detect duplication and unassigned responsibilities. | Forum register and dependency map. Show charter revision, effective period, chair, delegation reference and any decision limits. | COS approves the calendar and charter. Use the approved calendar, cutoffs and coverage requirements. | Validate time zones, conflicts, required specialist coverage, accessibility needs and contributor deadlines. Each option names affected venues and contributors with version and time-zone evidence. | Scheduling owner confirms the event or cancellation and authorized recipients. A deputy approves priority changes within delegation. |
| J3-03 **I** (S15-01/02) | A forum approaches its submission cutoff. Use the current charter and admitted branch submissions. | Link each agenda item to its paper and state whether it requests discussion, recommendation or decision. Flag stale, missing, unapproved and duplicate inputs. | Agenda and read-ahead index with the exact revisions, time of last verification and outstanding submissions. | Chair approves agenda. Contributing owners attest disputed facts. Send dependency findings to J5 and service issues to J6. |
| J3-05 **D** (S15-02) | A working group ends or forwards a matter. Keep abstention, absence and affirmative agreement distinct. | Chair-reviewed working-group record and recommendation with exact supporting artifact versions. The recommendation remains pending decision. | Chair validates the record. Secretariat transfers the packet to the delegated forum or returns it to the sponsor. |
| J3-06 **D** (S15-02) | A delegated official provides a decision. Use the authority register and the exact material considered. | Match identity, delegation, scope, exact revision and signed disposition. Secretariat confirms authoritative filing and planned distribution. A missing signature leaves the item pending. |
| J3-07 **I** (S15-01/02) | An accepted decision requires follow-up. Use the signed decision and local owner roster. | Extract actions into a draft ledger, flag unclear ownership or dates, link dependencies and compute overdue status. Reconcile duplicate events by decision and action ID. | Action ledger with owner, due date, acceptance criteria, dependencies, exceptions and acknowledgment history. | Action owner accepts the assignment. Preserve disputed or late updates and the relationship to signed changes. | Dated variance or closure recommendation with evidence references and status history. | The action owner submits completion evidence. | J3-09 **I** (S15-01/02) | Responsibility passes between administrative staff or coverage shifts. Incoming lead confirms receipt and ownership of open tasks. Unacknowledged items stay with the designated fallback owner. |
| J3-10 **I** (S15-01/02) | An office service outage, holiday, or staffing gap affects a routine administrative process. Use the approved business-continuity schedule and coverage policy. | Prepare a coverage brief, alternate intake channel and manual records reconciliation list. Separate known service state from reported status. | Office continuity note with alternate process, handoff owner, affected dates and later reconciliation steps. | Service or office owner selects the response. J6 attests service status, J1 confirms coverage, and records staff confirm recovered entries. |
| J3-11 **I** (S15-02) | A published internal record changes or contains an error. Use both revisions, the accepted decision and approved recipient scope. | Compare the revisions, draft a correction notice, locate dependants, and queue acknowledgment checks. Preserve prior copy and trace. | Correction with old and new IDs, change reason, release authority and received-by evidence. | Record owner approves the correction. The authority re-signs a material change to an outcome. Recipients acknowledge where policy requires. |
| J3-12 **I** (S15-01/02) | Staff-wide calendar review or records transfer. Use aggregate forum and action data and the applicable retention schedule. | Draft a review of missing outputs, duplicate meetings and overdue dependencies. Prepare an archive index and flag unresolved holds. | Administrative coordination review and retention-ready evidence index with aggregate definitions and exception list. | COS approves process changes. Records officer confirms retention or transfer. J7 receives approved improvements for lessons follow-up. |

## J5 institutional planning

The institutional sponsor confirms the problem and owns expected benefits. The assigned J5 policy
or programme lead owns integration. Contributors attest only their inputs. Applicable approval
boards or delegated officials decide within the authority in force. J8 or the comptroller provides
financial concurrence where required. Signing a business option never delegates authority for
commitments, purchases or deployment.

| ID and basis | Trigger and controlled inputs | Agent preparation and checks | Exact output and acceptance evidence | Human decision and handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| J5-01 **I** (S15-03/04) | A sponsor requests a new policy or administrative programme. Use the mandate, authoritative policy and applicable approval route. | Identify requested outcome, jurisdiction, constraints, decision owner and exclusions. Map each requirement to its source. Flag contradictory or expired mandates. | Intake record and draft terms of reference with exact policy references and unresolved questions. | Sponsor confirms the mandate and lead. Secretariat identifies review venues. Refer ambiguous authority to the institutional policy or legal office. |
| J5-02 **D** (S15-04, where PAD applies) | Sponsors initiate a business case or existing case needs reconsideration. Use approved need statements and baseline evidence. | Assemble case rationale, affected groups, potential benefits and available capacity. Label forecasts and estimates. Identify which stakeholders were consulted. | Draft business case and benefit baseline with evidence, target dates, metrics and designated owners. | Sponsor validates the objective and benefit owner. J8 validates financial input. Applicable board or delegate makes disposition. |
| J5-03 **I** (S15-03) | Evidence arrives or a planning assumption approaches review. Use branch inputs, source versions, assumptions and evidence dates. | Classify facts, assumptions, and unresolved dependencies. Track an owner and review date for each assumption. Flag when new evidence contradicts or supersedes it. | Versioned evidence and assumption register with validation state, source cutoff and open items. | Each branch owner attests its inputs. The plan owner decides which uncertain assumptions can remain and records the rationale. |
| J5-04 **I** (S15-03/04) | A programme needs an administrative feasibility assessment. Plan owner resolves inconsistencies or escalates. |
| J5-05 **D** (S15-04, where PAD applies) | Sponsor seeks approval to charter a project. Use its mandate, resources, authority, benefits and review conditions. | Draft the charter, responsibility matrix and reporting expectations. Flag unassigned accountability, mismatched approval scope and dependencies awaiting owners. | Controlled draft project charter. Accepted version records sponsor, accountable owner, plan boundary, dates, benefits and approval. | Sponsor and applicable authority sign. Secretariat files the accepted version. Project management office receives the baseline. |
| J5-06 **I** (S15-03/04) | Sponsor asks for comparison of administrative alternatives, such as timing for a document-system migration. Use sponsor-approved evaluation criteria, accepted constraints and branch input. | Explain each alternative using documented criteria and provenance. Compute sensitivity to explicit assumptions. Keep unresolved trade-offs separate from scores. | Decision comparison showing criteria, weights, assumptions, material differences, dissent and what evidence would change the result. | Sponsor confirms criteria. Appropriate authority selects or declines. Agents cannot assign authority or settle contested weights. |
| J5-07 **D/I** (S15-04) | An applicable risk or complexity review is due. Use risk controls, impact evidence and subject matter input. | Draft a risk and dependency register with named reviewers, triggers, mitigations, privacy and accessibility concerns, and residual uncertainty. Capture administrative continuity and fallback ownership. | Risk register and draft assessment inputs with source, accountable owner, dated review and proposed treatment. | Risk owner accepts treatment within authority. Privacy, accessibility, security and finance specialists attest their areas or refer exceptions. |
| J5-08 **D** (S15-04, where PAD applies) | A chartered initiative needs an implementation plan or transition update. Use the accepted baseline and resources. | Draft milestones, owners, acceptance dependencies, communication, reporting, accessibility and administrative handoff. Flag unmet conditions instead of scheduling them as facts. | Versioned project management plan or administrative implementation plan with dependency and change history, and outstanding approvals. | Programme lead endorses feasibility. The designated authority approves baseline changes. J3 tracks only accepted administrative actions. |
| J5-09 **I** (S15-02/04) | A proposed policy or project decision is ready for a governance venue. Use charter, brief, current branch attestation, risk and benefit evidence. | Draft the decision packet with the decision requested, current artifact revisions, approvals still due, alternatives and dissent. Check that required review occurred for this exact packet. | Board submission manifest and dated artifact set, including concurrence status and incomplete evidence. | Chair accepts the packet for agenda. Delegated authority decides and signs. J3 records accepted follow-up. |
| J5-10 **I** (S15-03/04) | A decision is signed or a baseline varies materially. Use the signed disposition, old and proposed baselines, and impacted commitments. | Connect approved decision to exact changes, identify dependant artifacts and prepare an update with reasons and unresolved discrepancies. | Change register, accepted baseline or change request, revision diff and acknowledgment status. | Change authority approves or defers the update. Owner coordinates reapproval when scope or evidence changes. Dependant owners acknowledge. |
| J5-11 **D/I** (S15-04) | A scheduled programme review is due or benefits vary. Use agreed metrics and latest checked evidence. | Separate forecast from observed outcome. Sponsor decides follow-up. J8 attests numbers where applicable. |
| J5-12 **D** (S15-04, where PAD applies) | An initiative reaches closeout, transfer or termination. Use agreed acceptance criteria, outstanding obligations, retained documents and lessons. | Reconcile obligations, confirm responsible successor, draft the closeout checklist, and prepare the evidence index and referral of remaining items. | Project closeout record or checklist with remaining actions, owner acceptance, validated benefit status and accountable evidence custody. | Sponsor and closeout authority decide closure. J8 confirms relevant administrative commitments. J7 receives approved lessons and records officer accepts custody. |

## J7 education, training administration and lessons

The learning authority sets scope and objectives. A content owner verifies approved material.
Administrative completion and independently assessed competence are recorded distinctly. For
applicable Canadian lessons records, an authorized official submits to the Defence Lessons
Learned System (DLLS) in accordance with local policy. Agents prepare an export rather than
silently modifying a repository. A completed task does not count as a validated lesson without
evidence and the responsible authority's decision.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| J7-01 **D** (S15-01/07) | Annual planning or an approved change in administrative standards. Use accepted policy, role profiles and prior learning evidence. | Match approved administrative competencies to roles. ## Skills and tools to furnish

The proposal groups these tools by capability rather than vendor. Each deployment needs a named
connector owner, source purpose, permissions and a clear path for corrections. Preserve evidence
and review when tools change. Prefer read access to authoritative sources and write access only
to a designated draft or work queue.

| Staff team | Skills to evaluate in a portable fixture | Tools, least-privilege operations and expected evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J3 coordination agent | Meeting and agenda design, dependency recognition, attribution and minutes, role-aware authority checks, status reasoning, concise handover, time zones and accessibility | Calendar and roster read limited to authorized offices. Scoped agenda and draft action tools with versioning. Consent-aware notes intake. Decision register read and controlled draft creation. Approval verifier, status ledger and internal recipient resolver. Tool results show source revisions and unresolved conflicts. |
| J5 policy and programme agent | Policy interpretation and tracing, structured requirements, assumption management, comparison and sensitivity, arithmetic, change impact, benefits tracking, and precise decision requests | Read-only policy, programme register and authorized branch inputs. Controlled project templates, transparent spreadsheet calculations, change and dependency graph, and evidence-linked risk and benefit registers. Queue drafts for human concurrence. Show formula inputs, source cutoff, assumptions and conflicts alongside results. |
| J7 learning administration agent | Objective mapping, accessibility and plain language, version management, cohort and prerequisite reconciliation, observable administrative assessment, aggregate reporting | Scoped LMS reads and catalogue draft connector. Controlled syllabus and rubric store. Approved templates and synthetic-only scenario fixtures. Approved aggregate metric reporting. Show authoritative LMS timestamp and the difference between completion and competence. Leave grade, qualification and personnel changes to the authorized owner. |
| J7 lessons support agent | Observation normalization, evidence classification, duplicate assessment, cause and alternative reasoning, corrective-action tracking, privacy-aware export, and closure validation requirements | Trackable observation and corrective-action stores. Read-only artifact references and transcript intake where consent and scope permit. Controlled search and duplicate suggestions with human confirmation. Draft export with filtering and policy checks. Track acknowledgment and destination receipt separately. |
| Shared verification and release | Source freshness, scope and least privilege, record retention, formal and accessibility checks, dispute-preserving revision control, and side-effect discipline | Policy gate, authorized signer lookup, trusted clock evidence, source and artifact version identifiers, approved disclosure labels, immutable audit and retry-safe draft operations. No shared omnibus account. Publication uses the organization’s existing approval and release channel. |

Useful evaluation cases cover ambiguous delegation, missing or incompatible versions, both sides
of a documented disagreement, revoked permissions between draft and send, private observations
appearing in group summaries, incomplete implementation being reported as closed, and disconnected
replay of completed requests. Each evaluation checks the durable state, the precise human handoff,
and the absence of unintended alerts, publication or source-system writes.
