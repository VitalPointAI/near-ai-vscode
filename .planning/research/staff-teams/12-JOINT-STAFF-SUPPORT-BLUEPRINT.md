# Joint staff support blueprint

## Purpose and scope

This blueprint defines the human and agent work needed to produce institutional staff outputs.
It covers administrative coordination, information stewardship, ordinary business services,
resource management, civilian liaison, education, lessons and specialist advice support. It
sets out the evidence, tools and human authorities needed to move each product from intake to
controlled release. It is a proposed SovAI design. Local organizations establish actual duties,
delegations, source access and release authority through their terms of reference and policies.

The agent charter excludes operational military planning and execution, targeting, weapons,
operational intelligence collection, combat instruction, and offensive cyber activity. Where a
source describes a broader branch remit, the inventories cover the institutional staff-support
part of that remit. Activities outside the charter route to the appropriate human authority.
For this institutional design, the blueprint and duty inventories define the proposed agent
provisioning scope. Earlier staff research provides context and does not supply an agent grant.
An administrative part of a mixed request can proceed only if its purpose, inputs, audience
and effects are independently authorized.

The inventories use D for a supported function and I for an inferred workflow. Those codes
describe the relationship between a duty and its source. Establish source provenance separately.
Directly checked primary text, a citation traced through a prior repository note, and historical
background have different evidentiary weight. A historical or repository-traced claim requires a
local doctrine owner to confirm applicability before it informs a real role or delegation.

Existing staff research cites historical JP 3-33 and FM 6-0 for staff organization, staff events
and delegated boards. The relevant branch inventories distinguish indirect citations from a
directly checked source. A locally named file with a conflicting substantive title stays out
of normative citations until its identity and edition are confirmed. The research file covering
civil-military cooperation is used for liaison process and permissions only.

## Human and agent accountability

| Role | Responsible for |
| --- | --- |
| Accountable decision owner | Accepts, defers or declines the exact proposed action within a current delegation. Signs the decision and owns the outcome. Only one role is accountable for that decision. |
| Producing human | Owns the product, defines purpose and sources, checks omissions and conflicts, consults the required specialists and presents the version for decision. Can require corrections or discard an agent draft. |
| Authority or subject reviewer | Attests only the fields or criteria within their responsibility. A source owner attests evidence, a comptroller attests financial figures, and a security or privacy reviewer owns their respective clearance. |
| Release authority | Verifies recipient, current scope, handling, exact approved version and permitted channel. Signs release where required and sends or authorizes the exact copy. Approval of a decision and permission to distribute can be different authorities. |
| Receiving owner | Confirm receipt, accept the version within their remit and carry out the assigned human action. Acceptance evidence is distinct from a sending receipt. |
| Records custodian | Assign the authoritative record and version, verify retained evidence, manage authorized holds and disposition. |
| Lead branch agent | Reads approved inputs, checks currency and completeness, creates clearly identified drafts, and routes a draft for an authorized review. It records missing evidence and conflicting facts. |
| Independent checking agent | Reconciles sources, verifies references and checks structure, handling proposals, accessibility and consistency. Its result is input to human review. It receives the minimum evidence needed for its task. |
| Workbench and policy services | Enforce per-action authorization and resource boundaries, associate the draft with its evidence, and keep a trace of permitted state transitions. They require valid current authority independently of the agent's recommendation. |

For per-person administrative decisions, default to document assembly, completeness checks
and human routing. A workflow that evaluates an individual or contributes to a determination
requires its own organizational assessment before deployment. Avoid automatically ranking
people, assigning outcomes, issuing entitlement decisions or changing the authoritative record.

## Product contract and workflow

Each instance carries a duty ID from an inventory and a product ID that survives revision.
At intake, record the purpose, product owner, single accountable decision role, due date,
sources, scope, permitted tool effects and routing restrictions. For each consequential fact,
capture the source identifier and custodian, version or digest, time observed, time effective,
the relevant passage or data field, and its handling and freshness requirements. Use explicit
states for missing, stale, disputed, unsupported and not applicable. Preserve dissent and
distinguish observed facts, inference, estimate and direction. Never stand in a missing date,
authority or clearance with a default.

| Transition | Controller | Minimum evidence and behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake to assigned | Human branch owner or pre-authorized staff routing | Applicable duty, purpose, product boundary, authorized owner, evidence location and real decision role. Unknown authority stays in assignment review. |
| Assigned to draft | Branch producer and authorized agents | Checked access at source, fact-to-evidence mapping, original labels and draft version. A request receipt permits work only within its scope. |
| Draft to review | Human producer | Reviewed version, decision sought, source freshness, unresolved issues, required reviewer roles and handling. Missing evidence remains visible. |
| Review to decision | Authorized specialists and decision owner | Each review binds its exact version, scope and decision point. Permission or evidence changes hold the item for revalidation. |
| Decision to staged release | Human release authority | Exact approved version and attachments, destination and current recipient authorization, approved disclosures and any separate release permission. A signed decline or defer also gets a record. The exact release scope is single-use, can expire, and remains subject to revocation. |
| Staged release to delivered | Authorized human or an explicitly approved deterministic release channel | A deterministic transport may execute a prior exact human release authorization. Recheck current authority, version, recipient, purpose, domain and channel at delivery and retry. Reconcile uncertain transmission before retrying. Keep a minimal verifiable receipt or unresolved exception. |
| Delivered to closed | Named receiving owner and records custodian | Require receiving acceptance, completed action evidence, and authoritative custody assignment. A delegated authority can explicitly close an unfulfilled item with its disposition and reason. Refused or uncertain delivery stays open without that disposition. |
| Any pending state to held or superseded | Authorized human or refusal by the policy boundary | Record the reason and human owner. Keep the previous version intact. Resume only after the revised input, authorization and route match. |

An approval binds the specific product version, disposition, scope, authorizing position,
holder, the exact attachments and recipient, permitted purpose, destination, channel and expiry where relevant. A proposal, draft, reminder,
meeting record or signature reference alone does not create approval. Editing or replacing a
signed version requires revalidation. Retrying a failed handoff cannot silently broaden
recipients, duplicate official publication, or bypass record classification. A revocation after
delivery starts a human correction or recall decision. When policy requires distinct reviewers,
one person holding two titles meets that requirement only if the governing rule permits it.

## Capability inventory for agent teams

All skills below require a bounded tool manifest and synthetic acceptance evidence before use.
A branch inventory names the specialist tools. These common capabilities can be reused with
different source grants and output scopes. Broad accounts, general-purpose command execution
and unrestricted sending are outside the role bundles.

| Capability | Skills to provide | Tool contract and outputs | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source discovery | Apply scoped search, source authority, lexical vocabulary and freshness. | Read approved catalogue and document sources. Return reference, owner, policy, effective time and enough text for a cited claim. | Never indicate hidden source existence. Read authorization is checked at source. |
| Evidence validation | Compare exact versions, authenticity indicators, assumptions and conflicting records. | Read provenance and compare versions. Produce claim ledger, stale-source flags, unexplained deltas, dissent and source-quality review. | Agent confidence cannot replace missing evidence or issuer authority. |
| Structured drafting | Use branch templates, accessible prose, plain-language summaries and exact tables. | Write drafts in the branch workspace. Package required fields, attachments and request for decision. | No authority to mutate case systems, approval records, or signed artifacts. |
| Tabular analysis | Unit normalization, periods, baselines, sensitivity, reconciliation, group estimates and suppression. | Use sandboxed calculations over scoped datasets. Export formulas, denominators, assumptions and matched or unmatched rows. | Check cross-table inference. Do not treat a blank as zero or run hidden macros. |
| Agenda and actions | Scheduling, dependency checking, reviewer availability, handover and recurrence. | Read scoped calendars and write draft agendas or action proposals. Return conflict reasons and owner and deadline checks. | No calendar invitations, command directions or effects on official records without separately authorized workflow. |
| Review and annotation | Interpret signatures, version-bound approvals, classification discrepancies and exceptions. | Read decision and policy evidence. Prepare review packets, flag conflicting sign-offs and expose stale or revoked authority. | Agents cannot approve, sign, accept risk, sign for a human or certify completion. |
| Accessibility and official languages | Apply readable structure, accessibility checks, and bilingual equivalence. | Validate headings, tables, labels, alternatives, reading order and draft translations. Return discrepancies for qualified review. | Sensitive text goes only to approved translation routes. A qualified reviewer handles language and subject equivalence. |
| Handling and minimization | Preserve all source restrictions, identify re-identification and distinguish reference from content. | Check proposed label joins, excerpt scope, recipient and lost context. Prepare a redaction or release-assessment draft. | A stricter source restriction stays effective. Downgrading and release require authorized humans. |
| Records and continuity | Know official repository, preservation, hold, correction and supersession. | Read hold state and draft archives, indexes, acknowledgments, correction notices and handover reports. | Preserve existing holds. Disposition and official deletion stay with records authorities. |
| Defensive service evidence | Interpret approved availability, patch and recovery evidence, and interfaces. | Read scoped status and inventory tools. Draft evidence packets, remediation proposals and handoff to a human service owner. | No production shell, arbitrary network probing, entitlement grant or changes to infrastructure. |
| Publication staging | Maintain exact version binding, recipient validation, receipts and retry behavior. | Stage a reviewed copy and preserve retry and receiving evidence for the authorized release owner. | No external contact, posting, inter-domain transfer or mass sending from general agent roles. |
| Evaluation and governance | Trace evidence, test documents as untrusted input, evaluate authority changes, and look for hidden joins. | Run synthetic role fixtures and produce traceable pass, fail or needs-review results. | Independent review needs scoped evidence. No fabricated approval, authority or test claim. |

## Provisioning status and build dependencies

The codebase has staff release, project versions, policy and role-resolution foundations. The
local workbench's self-declared actor is useful for a test. Shared-position resolution checks
who holds a recorded decision role. Production delivery also needs independently trusted
identity and authority, destination checks, source-specific routing, review evidence and a
receipt. Use the existing staff release path for the internal decision record, and record
external delivery and receiving acceptance alongside it. The delivery channel executes a
specific human release authorization.

| Role bundle | Reuse once verified for the workload | Additional proposed tools and checks | Accountable tool owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Command Group, J3, J5 and J7 | Staff drafts, project versions and human review | Read-scoped calendar or curriculum adapters, action and lessons evidence, signed exact-version decisions and correction receipts | Local coordination product owner, education authority for training sources |
| J2 stewardship and J6 business IT | Provenance records, classification and routing foundations | Approved metadata/source filtering, evidence and defensive service adapters, integrity and access rechecks | Data steward or service owner, with security and records review |
| J9 and Public Affairs | Draft and human review | Consent and accessible release packet, scoped delivery and receiving evidence. Initial use is draft-only | Liaison lead and separate release authority |
| J4 and J8 | Draft review and structured evidence | Ordinary inventory and finance read adapters, sandboxed reconciliation, independent evidence for receiving and financial decisions | Materiel data owner and comptroller |
| J1 and specialist advisers | Draft and review on public or synthetic data | Any named personnel-case, health, legal or complaint connector needs separate authority and assessment. Begin with public directories and approved aggregates | Responsible professional, privacy and records authorities |

## Cross-branch handoffs

| Producer | Receiver | Minimum shared product and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J1 personnel and J7 learning | Each other and J8 where authorized | Share aggregate eligibility and training capacity with time and denominator. J7 provides verified course status. J1 owns person records. Finance receives an authorized aggregate with its permitted use. |
| J3 coordination and J5 institutional planning | Chief of Staff and branch owners | Pass the exact signed decision, revised assumptions, due dates and dependencies. Keep draft recommendations, dissent and approvals separate. The receiving owner acknowledges the accepted revision. |
| J4 ordinary services and J8 resources | Contracting, stock custodian, finance | Pass contract reference, receiving evidence and relevant period or value. Receiving, certification, obligation and payment remain under their respective authorities. |
| J2 information stewardship and J6 service delivery | Each other and records/security authorities | Exchange minimum policy, metadata and custody evidence. Readers recheck source authority before opening the underlying item. Share a service exception without exposing protected content. |
| J9 civic liaison and Public Affairs | Approved partner, public channels or Command Group | Pass the exact approved draft, named source and consent scope, languages and expiry. Public Affairs or another delegated release owner decides what can be published. The liaison owner confirms partner acknowledgment. |
| All branches | J7 learning and records | Share a minimized observation or accepted correction with timeline, approved owner and validation criteria. Only the authorized lesson owner can mark a validated lesson and release it. |

Use one authoritative record for each forum. J3 prepares and maintains the shared calendar
(CG-03, J3-01 and J3-02), board packet (CG-05 and J3-03), action register (CG-04, J3-07
and J3-08), and minutes or disposition record (CG-08, J3-05 and J3-06). Command Group
or the chartered forum owns the direction and decisions within its delegation. Link each
accepted J5 assumption change or decision to the corresponding J3 action ID and signed
version. Cases A07 and A09 check this handoff.

Cross-branch permissions do not automatically cascade from one handoff to the next. Route a
metadata reference only if the recipient can learn of the item. Use a separate restricted lane for exception evidence. Keep case-level personnel, health, legal,
complaint, financial and other protected evidence in their proper service boundary. A
recipient with rights to view a report does not automatically get its named source records.

## Build order and acceptance

1. Start with synthetic Command Group and J3 decision and action records plus J7 lessons.
   They exercise the common version, authority, provenance and closure behavior without live
   organizational records or external delivery. Reuse the product registry, role-scoped
   `staff-products` actions and signed transition log. Add a duty mapping, claim-to-source
   version evidence, and restricted receiving-owner acceptance metadata tied to the product,
   hash and recipient. Verified delegation, authoritative source grants and delivery
   connectors are separate integration prerequisites.
2. Add read-only J2 and J6 stewardship and documentation, then J5 institutional estimates and
   J9 liaison drafts. Confirm source-level denial, deduplication, accessible and bilingual
   drafts, and safe route changes before enabling any organization connector.
3. Add ordinary J4 and J8 reconciliation against fictional ledgers and isolated structured
   datasets. Enforce segregation of functions, correct baselines, and transparent unit and
   currency treatment before requesting live read access.
4. Extend J1 and specialist admin only after the relevant accountable privacy, records and
   operational authorities agree on the data and use, and after a separate check of any
   administrative decision automation. Start with aggregate or public material.
5. For any production integration, require documented source owner, tool effect and data
   boundaries, approved manifests, traceable role evaluations, operator rollback, and the
   current authorization and deployment gates. Use the [assurance cases](16-joint-staff-support-assurance.md)
   to check which claims actually have evidence.
