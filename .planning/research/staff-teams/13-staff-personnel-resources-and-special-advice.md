# Personnel, resources and special advice

This catalogue assigns staff-support work to J1, J4, J8, the command group and personal or special staff. The accountable role in each row owns the staff product. A separate authority may have to approve an official record correction, personnel decision, expenditure or release. The local headquarters charter and current delegations determine who holds those roles. A smaller headquarters can combine functions, provided that it preserves independent reviews and protected case channels.

Each row has a stable identifier. **D** means an identified source supports the underlying staff function. **I** marks an implied support task or a design choice. Even in a D row, the agent, tool access, acceptance check and proposed workflow are product design. Source tags are resolved at the end. The example products are administrative drafts, evidence packs or approved aggregate extracts. Use approved fixtures and datasets. Inventory, cost and personnel examples cover ordinary institutional support.

The tool names describe capabilities to furnish and verify. A **reader** is a narrowly scoped view or versioned export of an authoritative system. A **draft workspace** can write proposed records in its own space. An **evidence reader** resolves an approved source or attachment without changing it. A **tabular checker** compares typed data in a sandbox. Availability, authorization and release are distinct. A proposal stays a draft until the designated human approves the exact version and the recipient scope.

**Handling.** Keep named records and personnel case files separate from the general branch workspace. Let the lawful custodian choose the approved classification, routing, recipient and aggregation rules. A small eligible aggregate can still disclose identity when joined with other releases. Check combinations, differencing and record history before export. Do not use a model to rank individuals or determine benefits, promotion, discipline, suitability or health status.

Validate these cases with fictional inputs before introducing a branch or specialist agent. A count sharing the same name and timeframe as a second extract must survive a disclosure check before release. An old authority must produce an unresolved delegation, even if the former signer is still in the distribution list. A contract statement that contradicts a signed receipt must keep the discrepancy open. A finance workflow must stop when the same person occupies roles that require independence. A protected legal attachment in a general brief must stay in its protected channel. An open retention hold or missing safety closure evidence must prevent proposed completion. A board decision that changed after staff assembly must be regenerated and reviewed against the new source version. Keep positive, refused and escalated test outcomes available for human review.
