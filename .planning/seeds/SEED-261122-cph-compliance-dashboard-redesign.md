# Compliance dashboard redesign

The owner is comfortable with the current dashboard for Phase 44 and wants a more visually engaging design in a future iteration, with graphics and animation.

## Design directions to explore

- Use charts and other graphics to make control coverage, evidence freshness, and gaps easy to scan.
- Explore animations that show what changed when evidence refreshes or a control status updates. Support reduced motion and keep the same information available without animation.
- Keep the underlying evidence, source links, and control details easy to reach from the visuals.

## When to Surface

Bring this seed into UI design when the roadmap next schedules work on the compliance dashboard. Choose the graphics, motion, and layout during that design pass.
