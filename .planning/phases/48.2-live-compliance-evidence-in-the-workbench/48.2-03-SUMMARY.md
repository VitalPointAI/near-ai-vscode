# Phase 48.2 Plan 03: Freshness, the tighten-only policy, the honest clock and layering in the one engine Summary

Every rule result now carries Current, Ageing or Out of date from its time, its window kind and the policy, by the same `isFresh` that makes a state Stale; a site policy can only shorten D-13's thresholds; a clock earlier than the release build shows every row with freshness Unknown and nothing Met; future-dated evidence is refused and not used; and a newer site observation replaces the release's result for its rules.
