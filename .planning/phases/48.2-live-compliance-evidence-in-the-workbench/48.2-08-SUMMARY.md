# Phase 48.2 Plan 08: The live site layer in the session Summary

The workbench backend now watches its read-only evidence mount: site files that land there are verified through your site key's trust document, layered over the release evidence, judged against the current time every 60 seconds and at every change, and handed to a waiting view through a 55-second long poll the moment the model changes; removed or unverifiable files drop their evidence at the next 30-second check, the README's projection is its own Not verified source, and nothing reaches the network.
