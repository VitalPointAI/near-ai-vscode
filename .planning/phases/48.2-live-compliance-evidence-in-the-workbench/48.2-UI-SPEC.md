# Phase 48.2 - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the Compliance view of the Sterling Sovereign AI (SovAI) workbench once it shows live, verified evidence: the source cards (release evidence, then your site's evidence, then the published projection when mounted), the four facts on every control cell (source, as-of time, freshness, verification) plus its evidence count, the summary across levels, the filters by state, freshness and source, every empty and partial state, live updates without losing place, and the screen-reader, keyboard and WCAG 2.2 AA behaviour. Produced by gsd-ui-researcher on 2026-11-25, to be checked by gsd-ui-checker. This file satisfies D-09 ("a UI design contract precedes the view plan") and the precondition of 48.2-10 Task 1.

Sources read: 48.2-CONTEXT.md (D-01 to D-30, LCE-01 to LCE-10, owner questions 1 to 6), 48.2-10-PLAN.md (in full), 48.2-08-PLAN.md (the view model, `getEvidence`, `waitForEvidence`, `getControlText`), the must-haves and behaviours of 48.2-01, 48.2-03, 48.2-04, 48.2-06, 48.2-07, 48.2-13 and 48.2-15; on `main` at 32007751: `workbench/extension/src/browser/sterling-compliance-module.ts` and `sterling-compliance.css` (today's filters, Needs attention buttons, donut, level chart, controls list, detail pane, focus handling), `workbench/README.md` ("Run locally", "Documentation and Compliance"), `sterling-portfolio-module.ts` (how a view reads `WorkspaceTrustService`), `.planning/phases/74.3-classification-assistant/74.3-UI-SPEC.md` (approved 2026-11-24: the original theme with lifted cards, the elevation tokens, the Times format), `.planning/research/coherence/GLOSSARY.md`, `src/test/copyLint.test.ts` and `src/test/helpers/copyLintScan.ts` (the copy lint's eight rules, its glossary words and compliance-claim words), `compliance/levels.json` (level titles).

Sketch for the owner's review (not shipped): `sketches/48.2-compliance-sketch.html`, self-contained, with a dark and light theme switch. It shows the full view at a site with layered, verified evidence and an open detail, then the partial states (fresh install with release evidence only, Restricted Mode, a single computer with no key, site evidence out of date, Did not verify, live updates paused, clock earlier than the release build, published projection only) in the original theme with lifted cards, with this contract's copy, sizes and spacing. ## Typography

Exactly 4 sizes and 2 weights. Today's view uses 11, 12, 16, 17, 21 and 30 to 48 px and the browser's bold; they map as shown.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line height | Used for | Replaces today's |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body | 13px | 400 | 1.5 | All running text, muted text, facts lines, badges, legend, table cells, buttons, fields, notices, the live indicator, the command block (editor font) | 11px, 12px and the default size |
| Heading | 16px | 600 | 1.25 | Source card titles, chart card titles, the Needs attention heading, detail component headings (`h3`), the control title in the detail pane | 16px, 17px |
| Title | 20px | 600 | 1.25 | Section headings (`h2`): "Evidence sources" and "Controls"; the detail pane's `h2` (the control). Card titles, including "Summary across levels" and "Control status", are Heading | 21px |
| Display | 28px | 600 | 1.15 | The page `h1` "Compliance" and the donut's centre count | `clamp(30px, 6cqw, 48px)` and `clamp(19px, 3cqw, 32px)` |

Weight 600 is also used for: the control id and name on a row, field labels, counts on the Needs attention buttons and in the legend, and table header cells. No other weight; `strong` is set to 600. No italics. Numbers use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Every text container has `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; nothing truncates except the command block, which scrolls. Copy tolerates 30% longer text (a later French pass) without layout change.

### Times

Every time is written plainly, in the fixed order of 74.3 "Times" (owner-confirmed 2026-11-24, 74.3 Q12): day, short month, year, 24-hour time and zone, for example "25 Nov 2026 14:02 UTC". Build it from `Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-CA', { day: '2-digit', month: 'short', year: 'numeric', hour: '2-digit', minute: '2-digit', hourCycle: 'h23', timeZoneName: 'short' }).formatToParts(date)` and join the parts in that order, so the browser's locale never changes it. Each time is a `time` element whose `datetime` holds the ISO value. Ages are plain words ("2 hours ago", "8 days ago") with the full time in `title`; an age never replaces the time on a card or in the detail pane, it follows it in brackets: "25 Nov 2026 12:01 UTC (2 hours ago)". A date-only value (a document's review date) is "25 Nov 2026".

Age words: under 1 minute "just now"; "1 minute ago", "{n} minutes ago" up to 59; "1 hour ago", "{n} hours ago" up to 47; then "{n} days ago". A time in the future within the 5-minute tolerance reads "just now".

## Layout (top to bottom)

1. **Header** (`.sterling-compliance-heading`): `h1` "Compliance" (Display); under it the lead sentence (Body, muted, max 68ch): "Live evidence for Sterling Sovereign AI, judged against the time now. Authorization is your organization’s decision." On the right, wrapping under the title below 680px: the live indicator (see "Live updates") and the secondary buttons for the connection state ("Pause live updates" or "Resume live updates", and "Check evidence now" with the `refresh` icon; see "Header buttons by connection state").
2. **Notices** (only when any; each a lifted notice card). One notice per kind, in this fixed order, with one exception for Did not verify:
   1. Did not verify (`data-tone="error"`): **one notice per failed source**, in source order (release, your site, published projection), each naming its source and its own reason. Two sources that fail for different reasons give two notices, never one combined notice, because each has its own reason and its own fix, and a combined sentence would have to drop one reason. The one exception is a shared cause: sources that fail with the same reason code share one notice that names them all (`notice.didNotVerify.shared`), so `anchor-mismatch`, which fails the release and your site's evidence together, gives one notice, not two identical ones. At most 3.
   2. Live updates paused, connection lost (`warning`): at most 1. Live updates paused by you has no notice: the header indicator and the "Resume live updates" button say it, because the person chose it.
   3. Clock earlier than the release build (`warning`): at most 1, shown only when at least one source verified.
   4. Your site's evidence out of date (`warning`): at most 1, shown only when the site layer verified.
   So the page holds at most 6 notices (3 Did not verify plus one each of the other three); a failed site layer rules out kind 4. Notices sit above the source cards; the scroll anchor (see "Live updates") keeps the viewport still when one appears or goes. The release Not verified guidance is not a notice: it lives in the release card (it is about that source).
3. **Evidence sources** (`h2`, Title) and the `-sources` grid: `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(280px, 100%), 1fr))`, gap 16. Cards in fixed order: Release evidence, Your site's evidence, Published projection (only when mounted). Under the grid, the release-only line when there is no site layer (see "States"), then the disclosure "How current each source is".
4. **Needs attention** card (only when any count is above 0): Not met, Partly met, Stale evidence, Out of date, Not verified. Each button filters; pressing a pressed button clears that filter.
5. **Filters** (`.sterling-evidence-filters`): Coverage level, Evidence state, Freshness, Source, Search controls, then "Clear filters". Grid `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(160px, 100%), 1fr))`, gap 8px 16px; "Clear filters" takes the next cell, aligned to the bottom (`align-items: end`) and the start of its cell (`justify-self: start`). An `auto` track cannot follow an `auto-fit` repeat, so the button has no track of its own.
6. **Charts** (`.sterling-evidence-visuals`, 2 columns `4fr 5fr` from 870px, 1 column below): the Control status card (donut plus legend) and the Summary across levels card (table).
7. **Controls** (`h2`, Title) with the count line "{shown} of {total} control entries", the list, "Show more controls", and the detail pane beside it (two columns from 650px when open; stacked, detail first, below).

The focal point is the source cards: they are the first lifted cards on the page and say what the evidence is, how it was verified and as of when, before any count is read.

- A `dl` of facts, `dt` muted above `dd` (`display: grid; gap: 4px 16px`, two columns from 480px card width).
- At most one inline message line (icon plus sentence, Body), for the source's own reason.
- At most one action row (secondary buttons only).

| Card | Facts (`dt`: `dd`) |

### Control row anatomy (the cell)

Each row stays one `button` (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` the detail pane), as today, laid out in two lines:

- Line 1: "{label} · {title}" (Body, weight 600) and the display-state badge right-aligned (wraps under below 430px).
- Line 2, the facts line (`.sterling-evidence-facts`, Body, muted, `display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; gap: 4px 16px`), five facts in this fixed order, each an icon plus words:
  1. Source: "Release", "Your site", "Supplier reference site", "Published projection", or two joined with " and " ("Release and your site") when the cell's results come from two sources.
  2. As of: "as of {time}" (the oldest result time in the cell, because the cell's freshness is its worst result's, D-12); omitted when the cell has no evidence.
  3. Freshness: "Current", "Ageing", "Out of date", "Unknown" or "No evidence".
  4. Verification: "Verified", "Not verified".
  5. Evidence count: "1 evidence item", "{n} evidence items"; omitted when freshness is "No evidence".

A row is never produced for a source that did not verify (D-18).

Applicability (44.1 hand-off H-13, added 2026-11-25): a component cell that the applicability map marks Not applicable to that component, with the map signed off, carries no source, as-of time, freshness or verification; in the detail pane it holds only `applicability.factsNone`, not "No evidence", and it is left out of every freshness and verification count. A row whose remaining components carry evidence shows the facts of those components only; a row in which every in-scope component is Not applicable is not a Not applicable row (see the next sentence). A row whose every component has no part reads "Your organization’s responsibility" with the engine's reason (`no-component-applies`), and its facts line is `applicability.noComponentApplies`. A component marked Not applicable but not yet signed off reads Not met as the engine says, with `applicability.unreviewed` in the detail pane, and keeps its four facts.

The row's accessible name is one sentence built by the pure helper in `common/compliance-evidence.ts`, starting with the visible line 1 (WCAG 2.5.3): "{label} · {title}. {display state}. Source: {source}. As of {time}. Freshness: {freshness word}{, reason}. {verification word}. {count words}." Example: "AC-2 · Account management. Met. Source: Release and your site. As of 25 Nov 2026 12:01 UTC. Freshness: Ageing. Verified. 4 evidence items."

## Live updates

### Behaviour (D-25)

- At open the widget calls `getEvidence()`, then loops `waitForEvidence(revision)` while attached; it stops on dispose and when the widget is detached (a closed tab polls nothing).
- A new revision re-renders in place with these kept: every filter value; the number of rows shown ("Show more controls" pages); the open detail pane (by the control's `{level}/{control}` key) and its scroll position and open disclosures; keyboard focus (by the same key on rows and by id on other controls); the scroll position of the widget. Scroll is kept by anchoring: before the update, record the key and viewport offset of the first row whose top is inside the viewport (or of the first card if no row is), and after it restore `scrollTop` so that element sits at the same offset. `overflow-anchor: none` on the widget so the browser's own anchoring does not fight this.
- A focused row whose control no longer matches the filters: focus moves to the next row in the list, or to the Controls heading (`tabindex="-1"`) when none follows. The open detail of such a control stays open with its new values and the line `detail.noLongerMatches` at its top.
- The search field is never re-rendered while it has focus; typing is never interrupted.
- No row flashes, moves with animation or is highlighted after an update. Row order follows the existing priority sort, so a changed state can move a row; the scroll anchor keeps the viewport still.
- When the long poll fails: the indicator shows paused, the "While paused" rules apply, and the widget retries after 2, 5 and 10 seconds, then every 30 seconds. The first success clears the paused state and re-renders.
- "Check evidence now" calls `getEvidence()` at once (also while paused by a lost connection), shows the result, and moves nothing; the button is `aria-disabled="true"` while the call runs and keeps its label.
- **"Pause live updates"** (WCAG 2.2.2; `debug-pause` icon) holds the shown values: the widget stops its long poll and applies no new revision. While held, the "While paused" rules apply with the words "Unknown (live updates paused by you)", the indicator reads "Live updates paused by you · Last updated {time}", and the button becomes "Resume live updates" (`debug-continue` icon). It is the same button element with a new label, so focus stays on it; the polite region says "Live updates paused by you." or "Live updates resumed.". "Resume live updates" calls `getEvidence()`, re-renders with the newest values (keeping place as for any update), and restarts the long poll. The hold lasts until resumed or until the view is closed; it is not stored, so reopening the view starts live.

### Header buttons by connection state

| State | Buttons, in order |
|---|---|
| Connecting | Check evidence now |
| Live | Pause live updates, Check evidence now |
| Paused, connection lost | Check evidence now (pausing a view that receives no updates would mean nothing) |
| Paused by you | Resume live updates ("Check evidence now" is not shown: fetching would break the hold the person asked for) |

The view's connection machine: Connecting, then Live; Live to Paused (lost) on a failed poll and back to Live on the first success; Live to Paused by you on "Pause live updates", and to Connecting, then Live, on "Resume live updates". A lost connection while held is noticed only after resume. No state waits forever: Paused (lost) keeps retrying every 30 seconds, and Paused by you always offers Resume.

### While paused (lost connection or by you)

Display states and every state count (donut, legend, the Met, Needs attention and Not verified columns, the Evidence state filter) stay as last judged. Everything derived from freshness reads Unknown, never the last value, because freshness changes with the clock and the view is no longer judging it:
- each row's freshness fact: "Unknown (live updates paused)" or "Unknown (live updates paused by you)";
- the release card's Vulnerability scan line: the time and age stay (they are facts); the freshness word becomes the same Unknown words;
- the site card's Items line: the same Unknown words in place of the three counts;
- the summary table's Ageing and Out of date cells, including the "All levels" totals: "–" (`aria-hidden="true"`) followed by the visually hidden words "Unknown (live updates paused)" (or "… paused by you"), so a screen reader never hears a dash;
- the Needs attention "Out of date" button is hidden;
- the Freshness filter keeps its value but is `disabled` and not applied, with the helper line `filter.freshnessPaused` under it (`aria-describedby`); it applies again on the first live answer.

`notice.clock` and `notice.siteOutOfDate` stay up while paused if they were up: both rest on times already received (a clock earlier than the build, a last collection time), and being out of date can end only with new evidence, which the view sees only after live updates run again. Neither notice is newly raised while paused.

### The "Updated" indicator

The live indicator (`.sterling-evidence-live`, Body, muted) sits in the header next to "Check evidence now":

| Condition | Icon | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Connecting (before the first answer) | `broadcast` | Connecting to live updates |
| Live, last answer under 1 minute ago | `broadcast` | Live · Updated just now |
| Live, older | `broadcast` | Live · Updated {age} |
| Paused, connection lost | `debug-disconnect` | Live updates paused · Last updated {time} |
| Paused by you | `debug-pause` | Live updates paused by you · Last updated {time} |

"Updated" is the time the view last received an answer from the backend, including a long poll that returned the same revision after 55 seconds: that answer confirms nothing changed, so a healthy view always reads "Updated just now" or "Updated 1 minute ago". The text is a `p` holding a `time` element (`datetime` = ISO, `title` = the full time). The visible age is refreshed every 15 seconds by a timer that only rewrites this text.

### Screen-reader behaviour

- The indicator is not a live region: its ticking age is never announced. It is read when the person reaches it, as its full text ("Live · Updated just now").
- One persistent, visually hidden polite region (`.sterling-evidence-announcer`, `role="status"`, created once at widget start, never re-created) carries every live announcement. Nothing else in the view has `role="status"` or `aria-live` during live updates; today's per-render `role="status"` on the count line is replaced by this region (the count line is announced through it only after the person changes a filter: "{shown} of {total} control entries").
- On a new revision, the view compares the old and new model and announces, at most once every 60 seconds (changes inside the window are merged into one message), and only while the widget is visible:
  - a source's verification changed: "{source name} is now {verification word}." (for example "Your site’s evidence is now Verified.");
  - cells changed display state, freshness or verification: "Evidence updated: {n} controls changed." ("1 control changed");
  - nothing the person can see changed: nothing is announced.
- Paused and resumed are announced once each: "Live updates paused." (connection lost), "Live updates paused by you." (after "Pause live updates") and "Live updates resumed.". These three are announced at once, outside the once-a-minute window, because they change how every freshness word reads. While paused by you nothing else is announced. Did not verify on any source is announced politely as "{source name} did not verify." (not `role="alert"`: the person did nothing, and the notice stays on the page).
- Focus is never moved by an update, except when the focused row leaves the list (see above).
