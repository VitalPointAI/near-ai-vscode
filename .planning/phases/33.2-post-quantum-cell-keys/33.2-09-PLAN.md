## Threat model

| Threat | Blocking verification |
| --- | --- |
| T-33.2-25. Partial or replayed evidence permits a write | Reject missing pages, invalid cardinality, duplicate keys and wrong-domain signatures or hashes. Check same-predecessor branches with distinct or equal revision IDs and reject stale or mismatched openings under the selected root. Check epoch, cell, writer and session binding. Keep the unaffected cell writable across normal sync and test bounded resume after delayed evidence. |
| T-33.2-26. Custom clearance policy diverges on another computer | Check signed results with custom scales and mappings before and after interruption, restart and sync. |
| T-33.2-27. Managed proof reveals confidential cell membership | Inspect common roster, unrelated cell delivery and blind-hub stored and request data for labels, recipients, protected cell decisions and unsalted identifiers derived from private state. |
| T-33.2-28. Interruption or concurrency exposes new content under an old key | Exercise staged and committed recovery, overlapping exclusions and both guarded signing paths, including prepared blob rejection. |
| T-33.2-29. A legacy client bypasses the switch | Test old builds and already-open sessions, fresh authorized history and blob recovery, migration restart, protected ingress and authorized draft recovery. |
