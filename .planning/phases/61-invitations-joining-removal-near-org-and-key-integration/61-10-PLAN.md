| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-61-40 | Elevation of privilege | member raises own cap | high | mitigate | Members are not org members and hold no session (D-21); only owner and admins can change limits on NEAR |
| T-61-41 | Information disclosure | clear key in memory or at rest | high | mitigate | Buffer only between create and seal and between open and install, zero-filled in finally; tests scan blobs and storage files |
| T-61-42 | Tampering | undelivered key left spendable | medium | mitigate | A refused append zeroes then revokes the new key; failures queue zero-limit and revoke-key items |
