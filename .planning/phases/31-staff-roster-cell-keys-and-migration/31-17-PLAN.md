Complete the authority rules of the single-writer roster in the driver (D-08 to D-11, D-48; owner decision 5): the deputy lead, lead handover, naming the deputy, roster splits and the person's choice, and holding entries that cite an unchosen branch.

The test helper is not edited in this wave.

Recorded planning choices: the deputy may choose a roster branch only when the lead's devices are all revoked or the lead has handed over (D-09 as confirmed by D-48); a lead who is merely unreachable is replaced through `lead.handover` signed by the deputy with reason `lead-lost`. Encoding of a choice [ASSUMED A12]: `roster.choose` cites the split point as its `rosterPrev` and names a child of it as `chosen`; it occupies one position, and the chosen child then applies at the next position with its own `rosterPrev` still the split point.
