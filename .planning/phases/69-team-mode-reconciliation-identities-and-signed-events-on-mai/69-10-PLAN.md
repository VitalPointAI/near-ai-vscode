Build the one signer and verifier every later signed thing uses (TMR-05): domain-separated, versioned, purpose-bound signatures over canonical JSON, checked against one key-status lookup that honours rotation and revocation, with the team roster as the first status source.

Purpose: Phase 70 signs policy bundles, Phase 71 signs grants (by people and by agents), Phase 80 signs approval answers; D-08, D-10, D-20 and D-21 settle the format now, before anything signed with it is kept. The local key history (the solo status source) is plan 11.
Output: `src/domain/team/signedPayload.ts`, `src/domain/team/keyStatus.ts` and their tests.
</objective>
