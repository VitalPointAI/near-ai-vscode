## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-32.1-N21 | Spoofing | a forged or replayed export claiming a newer release | high | mitigate | only exports signed by the listed site's own key, for its own site id, with `generatedAt` equal to the signing time and within 7 days count (Task 2); the edge refusal (32.1-31) still holds if a check is wrong |
