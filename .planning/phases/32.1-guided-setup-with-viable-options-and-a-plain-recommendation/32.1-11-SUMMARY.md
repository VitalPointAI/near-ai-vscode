**Get Started now has a pure state machine that validates page messages, previews the changes a person can apply, and resumes interrupted or partly applied setup.**

## Accomplishments

- The reducer orders the conditional questions, respects fixed and locked answers, and starts a fresh check when Q4 changes. It gates choices on current viability, exposes the 90-second fallback, saves pauses, records abandonment, and limits retries to pending changes.
- The review model groups additions, changes and removals. It keeps a higher label, points to Lower Label for an intentional reduction, and turns insufficient clearance into a rules proposal. Shared-project writes wait and connection removals ask for confirmation.
- The screen model presents option cards, consistent help, discovery and scan text, the stop reason, apply results and the policy repair message. The static page shell has one polite live region and a focusable heading.
