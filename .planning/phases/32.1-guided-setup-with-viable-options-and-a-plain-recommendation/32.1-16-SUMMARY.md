# Phase 32.1 Plan 16 summary

The Get Started discovery service composes local announcement sources, confirmed scanning and pinned endpoint proposals. The service is verified with fixture transports.

- A ready signed policy and matching network gate the announcement sources. Already listed or duplicate endpoints are skipped. New candidates undergo TLS pin collection followed by pinned signature requests. Candidates remain separate from the approved model options.
- Approval rechecks current policy, network, authority and the original pin before opening the policy editor. The editor retains authority to apply or export the proposal. The audit chain captures its outcome. A first delegated personal endpoint list retains inherited effective endpoints.
- Each scan consumes one displayed offer and uses its exact configured range and port tuple. It reports progress, records start and end, and aborts on Stop, cancellation, policy revocation, or a network change. Fresh network checks precede TCP, TLS, and pinned signature calls. The connector receives the remainder of the existing per-attempt timeout.
