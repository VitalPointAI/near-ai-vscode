<objective>
Build what lets a level that needs attested-cloud reach NEAR AI Cloud again, under the owner's CLAS-05 amplification: before sending, NEAR AI Cloud's attestation must bind the connection's TLS certificate to the enclave and the request's own connection must present that certificate; after each answer, the attestation and signature are checked, and a failed check stops that level for the chat until a person's Check again passes. A research step first records what NEAR AI Cloud's attestation exposes. Until this PR, plan 19's rule kept these levels on this computer (D-50).

Purpose: PR 4d (D-49). CLAS-05 as ratified on 2026-11-16 (verifying after a response proves which model ran, not that the connection was private) with D-36's after-answer half moved here from the first draft's plan 24 Task 1; CLAS-06 (no fallback, nothing sent on refusal); OFFL-01 (the decision reads only cached facts, every check runs out of band); GATE-07 (route.attestation-checked and route.attestation-cleared, on the lists since plan 19); CLAS-07 (SECURITY.md says what the checks prove and rely on). D-31, D-36, D-37 and D-55 hold; nothing here relaxes a tier.
Output: the NEAR AI Cloud binding source and the pinned transport in `connectionBinding.ts`, the shared binding check and the report cache in `attestation.ts`, the client and factory wiring, the after-answer check with its stop and Check again, the SECURITY.md text, `gateConnectionBinding.test.ts` and the after-answer cases in `gateRouteTiers.test.ts`.

Moved, not lost: the pure binding rule (the fact, `isBindingFresh`, `route.connection-not-bound`, the fail-closed refusals) and the client's `binding` option are in plan 19 (PR 4a); the first draft's plan 24 Task 1 is this plan's Task 2; the first draft's plan 24 attested-cloud paragraph and limitation row are in this plan's Task 3.

Size note: about 106,000 tokens, just over the advisory 100,000: the research finding, the source, the pin and the after-answer check only prove the amplification together, and 4d is the release that lets these levels reach NEAR AI Cloud again.

Recorded design choices (planner's discretion, visible): the fact is cached and read synchronously so OFFL-01 holds; the first refresh starts when a NEAR client on NEAR AI Cloud's own endpoint is created, so a labelled chat rarely meets an unchecked connection, and when it does the refusal says to try again in a moment; pinning uses `node:https` with `checkServerIdentity` because the global `fetch` exposes no certificate hook without a new dependency; the binding is proved per host, the pin per connection; the after-answer stop is in memory per chat (D-55).
