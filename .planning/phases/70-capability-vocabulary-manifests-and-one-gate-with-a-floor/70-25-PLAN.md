<objective>
Build the pre-send half of the owner's CLAS-05 amplification: a level that requires attested-cloud is sent to NEAR AI Cloud only when NEAR AI Cloud's attestation binds the connection's TLS certificate to the enclave and the request's own connection presents that certificate; otherwise the request routes local-only (allowed through a local model, refused with a plain reason when there is none). A research step first confirms what NEAR AI Cloud's attestation actually exposes and records it.

Purpose: CLAS-05 as ratified by the owner on 2026-11-16 (70-CONTEXT.md "Owner decisions after planning"): verifying attestation after a response proves which model ran, not that the connection was private. CLAS-06 (no fallback, refused with a plain reason, nothing sent) and OFFL-01 (the decision reads only a cached fact; the networked check runs out of band) hold. D-31, D-36 and D-37 keep their meaning; this plan adds a precondition to attested-cloud, never a relaxation.
Output: the binding fact in `src/domain/gate/routing.ts`, `src/services/connectionBinding.ts` (the source, the NEAR source and the pinned transport), the shared binding check in `src/services/attestation.ts`, the client and factory wiring, `gateConnectionBinding.test.ts`, the SECURITY.md text and its gateDocs check.

Recorded design choices (planner's discretion, visible): the fact is cached and read synchronously so OFFL-01 holds; the first refresh starts when a NEAR client on NEAR AI Cloud's own endpoint is created so a labelled chat rarely meets an unchecked connection, and when it does the refusal says to try again in a moment; pinning uses `node:https` with `checkServerIdentity` because the global `fetch` exposes no certificate hook without a new dependency; the binding is proved per host, the pin per connection.
</objective>
