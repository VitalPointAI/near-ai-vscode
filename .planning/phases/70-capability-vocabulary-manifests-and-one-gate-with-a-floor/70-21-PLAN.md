## Trust Boundaries

|----------|-------------|
| policy draft (person-edited JSON) -> the signed policy | the person can write any clearance into their own policy |
| draft file in global storage -> Apply Policy Draft | anything with disk access, or another window, could change the policy before Apply |
| docs -> people labelling content | an overstated guarantee leads to misuse |
