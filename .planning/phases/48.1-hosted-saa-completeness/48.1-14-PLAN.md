<objective>
The audit archive as code [AU-9, AU-9(2), AU-9(4)], tracer first: a daily export of the site's audit records to a write-once store with a signed, chained digest manifest, and an offline verifier for auditors (D-17). The signing key sits in OpenBao Transit under a policy only the archive job holds, separating it from cluster administrators and every human role.

Purpose: SAA-06 (the mechanism). 48.1-15 runs it at a site (image, chart, fit entries); 48.1-16 adds archive-before-delete, retention and its alerts.
Output: the two tools and their test, the OpenBao policy, role and key, the key inventory row.

Planning choices (reported):
1. Node built-ins only (fetch, zlib, crypto); the tools take no dependency, like the other deploy scripts.
2. Signatures are Ed25519 from Transit (`vault:v1:` prefix stripped for verification); the public key reaches auditors at the key ceremony (CONTEXT hand-off 1), never from the archive itself.
3. Write-once is the store's property (supplied by your organization, 48.1-15); the tool adds its own refusal to overwrite and the chain, so a store without object lock still shows tampering.
</objective>
