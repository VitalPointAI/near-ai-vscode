<objective>
The audit archive as code [AU-9, AU-9(2), AU-9(4), AU-11], tracer first: a daily export of the site's audit records to a write-once store with a signed, chained digest manifest; deletion from the online store only of days the archive verifiably holds (owner direction: deletion waits for the archive); an offline verifier for auditors; and a daily watch for signatures nobody expected (D-17).

Purpose: SAA-06 (the mechanism). 48.1-15 runs it at a site; 48.1-16 removes deletion from the OpenSearch retention policy, grants the archive job its delete right, adds the alerts and writes the documents.
Output: the tools and their tests, the OpenBao policy, role and key, the key inventory row.

Planning choices (reported):
1. Node built-ins only (fetch, zlib, crypto); the tools take no dependency, like the other deploy scripts.
2. Signatures are Ed25519 from Transit (`vault:v1:` prefix stripped for verification); the public key reaches auditors at the key ceremony (48.1-HANDOFFS.md, item to 47-17), never from the archive itself.
3. What the chain proves and what it does not: with a start anchor and an expected last day, it detects edits, removals, reordering, a wrong start and a missing tail. It does not stop someone who can sign (a cluster administrator acting as the archive's service account) from writing a new, validly signed day, or, on a store without a retention lock, from rewriting days. That is why the store must be write-once (48.1-15), why the signature watch exists, and why the residual risk has its own POA&M item (48.1-01).
4. One Kubernetes auth mount in OpenBao, created by 47-13 (for cert-manager); 47-24 adds only its `raft-snapshot` role, 47-33 only `offsite-manifest`, and this plan only `audit-archive`; nobody re-creates the mount (48.1-HANDOFFS.md H-09).
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| cluster administrators to audit records | people who run the site against the record of what they did |
| archive job to OpenBao | a workload identity asking for a signature |
| online store to deletion | the moment audit records leave the searchable store |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-48.1-34 | Tampering | archived audit days | high | mitigate | Signed manifests with SHA-256 per file and a chain between days; offline verification from an anchor to an expected last day (Task 1); write-once store (48.1-15) |
| T-48.1-35 | Repudiation | deleting, reordering or cutting off days | high | mitigate | Chain, recorded anchor and expected last day; verify reports a missing, swapped or truncated day (Task 1) |
| T-48.1-36 | Elevation of privilege | a person signing a forged manifest | high | mitigate | Sign-only policy bound to one service account; no human role holds it (Task 3, roles invariant) |
| T-48.1-37 | Spoofing | a cluster administrator running a pod as the archive service account | medium | mitigate | They could sign the current day's manifest only; archived days stay on write-once storage; the daily signature watch fails on a second signature (Task 2); residual risk recorded as a POA&M item for the authorizing official (48.1-01) |
| T-48.1-50 | Repudiation | online audit days deleted before archiving | high | mitigate | The job deletes an index only after verifyArchive passes for its day; tested with missing, unsigned and altered days (Task 2); no retention-policy delete on audit-* (48.1-16) |
| T-48.1-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | Node built-ins only; no package |
</threat_model>
