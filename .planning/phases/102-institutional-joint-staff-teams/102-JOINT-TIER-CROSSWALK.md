# Institutional needs at joint and strategic joint headquarters

In SovAI, L1 is joint and L0 is strategic joint. Both use the J family
where that fits local terminology. These product levels are independent of
national administrative reporting levels. Use the actual organizational
charter to name each owner. Directorates can be combined, absent, or named
for their function.

## Source profiles

- **United States.** Use JP 3-33 chapter II, figure II-3, for the L1 joint headquarters
  profile. Its example makes J7, J8 and J9 optional directorates. For the L0
  Joint Staff profile, use the institutional action coordination in CJCSI
  5711.01E, Enclosures B and C. A staff action package can record concurrence
  and nonconcurrence. Preserve both and keep decision authority with the
  designated person. [JP 3-33](https://www.jcs.mil/Portals/36/Documents/Doctrine/pubs/jp3_33.pdf)
  · [CJCSI 5711.01E](https://www.jcs.mil/Portals/36/Documents/Library/Instructions/CJCSI%205711.01E%20CH1.pdf)
  · [Office of the Director, Joint Staff](https://www.jcs.mil/Leadership/Director-Joint-Staff/)
- **NATO.** Use the applicable joint headquarters staff structure for L1.
  The official Brunssum position description uses J8 Finance with a
  Financial Controller. For the L0 International Military Staff profile,
  use the named divisions and offices. Its Human Resources Office,
  Logistics and Resources Division, Financial Controller and Information
  and Knowledge Management Office support separate role mappings.
  [NATO Brunssum Finance Directorate](https://www.nato.int/content/dam/nato/vacancies/pdf/Director%20of%20Finance%20-%20JFC%20Brunssum.pdf)
  · [NATO structure](https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/organization/nato-structure)
- **Canada.** The Canadian Joint Operations Command profile can name an
  L1 joint headquarters. For L0, the Strategic Joint Staff publishes
  Coordination, Support and Strategic Initiatives functions. Map the
  institutional duties to authorized Canadian offices using the NATO
  baseline selected for 2026–27. Keep national delegations and records
  separate from the baseline. [Canadian Joint Operations Command](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/organizational-structure/canadian-joint-operations-command.html)
  · [Canadian Strategic Joint Staff](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/organizational-structure/strategic-joint-staff.html)

## Proposed institutional outputs

These are reuse candidates for the existing duty families. The local terms
of reference determine the applicable approvals and named authorities.

| Family | L1 joint | L0 strategic joint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Command Group and J3 | A joint administrative board packet, versioned minutes and action handoff | A senior decision brief with separate office concurrence and dissent, decision record and disposition tracker |
| J1 | Consolidated office establishment and training capacity from approved aggregates | Policy and establishment reconciliation with service-level source owners and a named approving office |
| J2 information stewardship and J6 enterprise IT | Source catalogue, service exception and authorized metadata exchange | Governed terminology, provenance and digital policy evidence for multiple offices |
| J4 ordinary services and J8 resources | Ordinary shared-service receipts, period reconciliation and invoices for separate receiving and financial roles | Corporate service and budget evidence by reporting owner, with explicit financial controller review |
| J5 institutional planning | Joint administrative initiative brief, dependencies and handoff to the named sponsor | Versioned institutional policy options and decision trace, with source and concurrence evidence |
| J7 | A lesson review packet assembled from approved local observations | A minimized cross-office lesson synthesis, named validation owner and approved dissemination copy |
| J9 and Special Staff | Civilian liaison contact metadata, referral drafts, confidentiality and consent | Institutional liaison packet and specialist advice routing with national custody and release requirements |

## Acceptance needed for the joint tier

1. Select and reopen L1 and L0 separately while both display J. A level
   change invalidates a draft route relying on the old configured role.
2. Select a profile with a combined office and one with an unstaffed
   optional directorate. Route only to verified local positions. Keep an
   unmapped duty held for a human assignment.
3. Produce synthetic administrative coordination evidence at both levels.
   Preserve dissent, exact document versions and source-specific visibility.
   Distinguish office concurrence, human decision, release and receipt.
4. Move an approved aggregate from an L1 test office to an L0 review
   fixture. Verify authorized audience and purpose, and keep the underlying
   person-level and case-level material in its own source scope.
