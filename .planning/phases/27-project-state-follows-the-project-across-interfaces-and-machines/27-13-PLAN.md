<objective>
Sync archived threads without paying for them on every machine (D-08, flag 7): push archives as cold objects, fetch them lazily through a partial-clone mirror, merge a thread archived on one machine and continued on another with nothing lost, consolidate hot objects safely, and apply plain-words size limits.

Purpose: "never deleted" and "follows the project" require archives to leave the machine; bandwidth and size require them to be fetched only when opened.
Output: changes to stateSync.ts, stateTransport.ts, stateRecords.ts, threadStore.ts, threadArchive.ts, and two test files.
</objective>
