# Unclassified and Protected A evidence and implementation

1. Partition the remaining Unclassified and Protected A controls by control family and owning component. Preserve release history. For every unresolved row, record an existing supported implementation, missing evidence, confirmed product deficiency, organization or inherited action, or approved tailoring decision. Link known gaps and Phase 44.1 triage IDs.
2. Deliver independent control batches in existing components. Wire each evidence rule to the smallest exact test or sanitized operating evidence for the behavior. Separate product contribution, department duties and inherited settings. Split the remaining Protected A queue by control family and component after triage. Schedule only batches with an owner, affected controls, and a source or test for the claimed behavior.
3. Verify supported behavior in the integrated release evidence pipeline. Check that missing, stale, scope-dependent, and proven controls are reported accurately, including limitations and the applicable deployment evidence needed. Leave unconfirmed sponsor scope and approvals open.

Completion requires an owner and credible evidence or a linked action for every control in the approved scope. A gap in product implementation closes when the capability works and its reviewed evidence can be retrieved for the release and assessment.
