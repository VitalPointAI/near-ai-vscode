# Protected B, the Classified appliance and applicable overlays

1. Confirm the deployment and data scope with the sponsor. Identify applicable baselines, overlay triggers, inherited controls, and evidence handling rules. Preserve Classified reference-only rows until the appliance and its authority are assessed.
2. Apply the same per-control triage and implementation checks used for Phase 44.1-02. Map product controls to the relevant release and components. Track decisions that require a departmental or other external authority.
3. Review each applicable profile and overlay against the approved boundary with the sponsor and assessor. Verify scope-specific coverage, evidence references, and unclosed actions.

For a submission scoped to Unclassified or Protected A, record the sponsor's confirmed exclusion or deferral of these other profiles and overlays. That record satisfies this plan's scope check for that submission. Product work on verified requirements and unrelated release work can continue in parallel.

## Supplemental evidence paths

The local register gives each dashboard objective in ITSP.10.171, CPCSC Level 1, Controlled Goods, and ITAR/EAR an assessment path with a responsible role, Phase 44.1 milestone, and evidence or applicability decision. Scope each action to its own profile. A shared objective ID cannot carry evidence or close actions across profiles unless each assessed scope supports that result. The local register keeps unallocated or withdrawn source identifiers open for authority reconciliation. Obtain the sponsor's approved scope, responsible officials, inherited-control evidence and approved handling arrangements before a submission claims coverage.

Classified remains a reference profile. The sponsor and responsible security authority establish the appliance and information boundary, applicable profile and evidence channels before assessment. The assessor reviews the appliance evidence and the sponsor records a signed applicability or deferral decision for the release or submission scope. The reference statements are retained in the assessor package.
