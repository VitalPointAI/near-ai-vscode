# Complete the deployment submission

1. Use the assessed build, component inventory, configurations and sponsor-supplied records to populate the applicable report and form fields. Validate the categorization, tailoring, ownership, evidence handling and authorized submission route. Document rationale and approval for any inapplicable field or form.
2. Create and review deployment architecture, trust boundary and data flow diagrams, including roles, environments, external interfaces, data handling, operational paths and dependencies. Place diagrams and relevant dated visual evidence in the report or cite the approved restricted evidence location by ID. Check each diagram against deployment inventory and reviewed configurations.
3. Reconcile the Word report, supporting forms, evidence index, traceability matrix, risk register and remediation actions. Record independent assessor findings, sponsor concurrence and approval or the department's verified submission and decision routing. Check all required entries, artifacts, signatures, distribution markings, revision and submitted archive.

Open and render the editable Word report. Verify that diagrams are legible and identify the assessed build, component inventory and configuration records. Reconcile the required-fields and attachments checklist with the package manifest and verify the archive hash. Assess submission readiness against the sponsor's actual intake requirements and record authorization separately if the decision is pending. The package remains a working draft until the required entries and evidence have been reviewed.
