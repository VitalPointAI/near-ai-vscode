**Archive.**
- 28 entries: `README.md`, `SHA256SUMS`, `SA-and-A-sponsor-draft.docx`, the evidence workbook (`.xlsx`), and the coverage, control traceability, component evidence, evidence index and gap register CSVs.
- All 27 `SHA256SUMS` lines verify against the entries.
- The release archive hash itself was not checked: it needs a real published release archive.

**Parser.** The export accepted the new reasons and the `applicability` field without change.
- The component evidence CSV carries the 3,800 excluded cells, each with its own "Not applicable to this component: ..." reason.
- The control traceability CSV carries 108 rows with "No component in this level's scope has a part in this control" (66 at Protected A, 42 at hosted Protected A).

**Coverage table** (`evidence/coverage-summary.csv`):
- "Unassigned deployment evidence paths" is 0 for every profile except **hosted-itsp-10-171: 131**. This is the finding in 44.1-03.
- "Unassigned product gaps" is 1 at Protected A and 1 at Protected B: ac-06, which has a statement but no results without test reports.

**Word draft.** `SA-and-A-sponsor-draft.docx` opened and rendered in LibreOffice (headless) to **226 pages**. It names the product, the release and the commit.
