<objective>
A passkey confirmation signs exactly one action: the one it was started for and whose summary the person saw (D-09, D-13). Three earlier fixes patched the binding case by case, and each was found to rest on a heuristic. This plan replaces them with one invariant, carried through the identity provider round trip by a backend nonce. It also keeps the engine's one append path as the only place a person-key kind is signed.

Purpose: a passkey confirmation means "I am doing this one thing I can see", never "whatever my session asks next".
Output: the action id on the asynchronous signer, the memory-only pending-action registry, the step-up summary and return routes, the append-path rule, and their tests.

Branch `feat/phase-33.1-17-action-step-up` from `feat/phase-33.1-positions` HEAD (D-06), merged back after wave 1. It needs 33-21, 33-23 and 46-02, which are on the base. It edits three 46-02 files additively. `common/session-identity.ts` gains a separate `actionStepUpUrl(nonce)`, while `stepUpUrl(currentPathAndQuery, acr)` and its tests stay unchanged. The backend module gains two routes beside the existing forwarded-token handling. `ActiveSession` gains the token's `sub`.
</objective>
