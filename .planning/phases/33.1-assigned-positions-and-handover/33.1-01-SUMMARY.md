Slots: `evidence` 1-16 sorted unique SHA-256 hashes; `status` 1-16 `{ list (line, 256), hash, validFrom }` sorted by list, unique; evidence and status always together; `did` is `did:webvh:` syntax, at most 255 characters; `basis { kind, ref? }` with kind in source, marking, guide, detector, compilation, judgement, ref a line of 1-128 characters, required for judgement.

Personnel family (only in the personnel key group, never in BASE_CELL_KINDS): `posting.unable { posting, report, sealed }` (report: a signed envelope `{ v: 1, purpose: 'staff-posting-unable', signer { fingerprint, publicKey }, signedAt, payload { posting }, sig }` naming this posting and carrying no reason; sealed: opaque base64url, at most 4,096 bytes; `unableReasonProblem` checks the opened `{ reason, text? }`: six reasons, text at most 500 characters); `posting.reference { posting, sealed }` (at most 2,048 bytes).

Actor: `{ kind: 'person', position?: <id> }`; agent and hub actors never carry a position.

Observer shapes of the contract (cell.marking, marking.approve, observer.approve, observer.release, member.invite observe, member.admit firstKey, the observer family) are written by 33.1-18 against the same answer.
