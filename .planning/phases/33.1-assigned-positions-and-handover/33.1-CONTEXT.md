# Phase 33.1 context

## Scope

Add positions and signed postings to the roster and hub established in Phase 33. Reuse the Phase 41 organization modes, terminology and person identity. The Staff Center consumes scoped position and presence data. This phase supplies the position-specific workspace contract. Order issuance, governed Personnel imports and the full planning cycle retain their own scope.

## Decisions

- **D-01:** An organization-owned position outlives its holder. A stable person identity can hold several positions through distinct postings. Use the verified roster principal now and resolve its DID once Phase 71 provides the binding.
- **D-02:** Only an authorized tasker can post someone. Enforce signed delegation, scope, effective dates and the assignment-mode flow. Device confirmation establishes key delivery. The assignee can report for duty or report unable, and the tasker decides any exception.
- **D-03:** A handover changes effective authority and access keys at the same roster transition. Acting and temporary postings respect the single-effective-holder invariant. Keep historical signer, position and decision attribution after handover.
- **D-04:** A person opens tasks, drafts, reviews and approvals scoped to one active position. Switching positions cannot carry another posting's authority. Recheck the current posting at protected actions and remove expired or relieved access. Respect separation of duties for dual-hatted people.
- **D-05:** Reuse the authorized hub presence projection. Presence is session activity. Verify fresh, expired and disconnected states independently of posting authority. Preserve parity for existing invitation-mode projects and refuse unsupported roster features via POST-01.
