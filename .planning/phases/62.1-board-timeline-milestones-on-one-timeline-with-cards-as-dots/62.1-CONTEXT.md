# Phase 62.1: Board timeline - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-04
**Status:** Ready for UI design contract and planning
**Source:** owner request and decisions in conversation (2026-11-04), in place of a discuss session

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A second view of the Team Board: one timeline for the whole project. Milestones are the major sections, phases are segments within them, and every card is a small dot placed in dependency order. Hover or click expands a dot into the full card. It reuses the board's model, permissions, actions and live updates; it adds no new signed events and changes no stored state.

Owner request (verbatim): "as part of the team board, I want a timeline visualization that shows the project milestones on one timeline (that user can view horizontally or vertically) and where each card falls in sequence (according to dependency) on that timeline. Card can be represented by a small circle or dot that when hovered or clicked on, expands into the full card. Use relevant animations to make it visually appealing."
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions (locked)

- **D-01 Sequence only (owner, 2026-11-04).** Position comes from dependency order, not dates. No planned or actual dates on the axis. Completion time, when known, is shown inside the expanded card only.
- **D-02 One timeline.** All milestones of the project on one axis, in roadmap order. Phases are segments inside their milestone. Cards (plans, board items and the planless phase cards from 62-19) are dots.
- **D-03 Dependency order.** Within a milestone, a dot's position is its dependency depth (longest path from a card with no unfinished dependencies); cards that can run in parallel share a step and stack across the axis. Ties break by phase then plan number. Cross-milestone dependencies are honoured. Cycles (should not occur) fall back to number order with a visible note. The layout is a pure, deterministic function in src/domain/team (no vscode import), tested on this repository's roadmap.
- **D-04 Orientation.** Horizontal or vertical, chosen in the board toolbar; a Columns | Timeline switch selects the board view. Both choices are remembered per person (webview state, versioned like 62-21).
- **D-05 Dots.** Small circles; state shown by colour AND a shape or icon (done, in progress, in review, blocked, held, ready, backlog, cancelled) so colour is never the only signal (WCAG). Assignee initials on or beside the dot. The current person's cards are distinguishable (Mine).
- **D-06 Expand.** Hover (and keyboard focus) grows the dot into a preview of the card and highlights its dependency lines in and out. Click (or Enter) expands it into the full card with the same actions as the board: Move to..., Assign to / Assign to me / Unassign me, details; Escape or clicking away collapses it. Actions go through the existing host messages and permission rules (no new paths).
- **D-07 Dependency lines.** Thin connectors between dependent dots, drawn faintly; emphasised for the hovered or expanded card.
- **D-08 Animations.** Dots appear in sequence on first draw; the expanded card springs open from its dot; dependency lines draw in; a dot pulses briefly when its state changes from a live update. All motion is disabled or reduced under prefers-reduced-motion. No animation blocks interaction; 60 fps target on the ~100-card repository board.
- **D-09 Scale.** Zoom and pan (keyboard, wheel and trackpad); finished milestones fold to a compact band (expand on demand); the current milestone is centred on open.
- **D-10 Accessibility.** Keyboard navigation along the sequence (arrows move to the next/previous step, up/down within a step), roving focus, screen readers get the timeline as an ordered list grouped by milestone and phase with each card's state and assignee; focus returns to the dot after the card collapses.
- **D-11 Real-window proof.** Same standard as the 62 fix pass: layout probe checks (no overlap, nothing clipped, readable expanded cards) and screenshots in both orientations, four themes and several widths, captured on the hidden virtual screen (xvfb-run with WAYLAND vars stripped), plus the code-server browser test for hover/click expand, for the owner to review.
- **D-12 Plain language, no developer terms, theme colours only, no new dependencies** (no charting libraries: SVG/CSS by hand in media/board/).

- **D-13 "Now" indicator (owner, 2026-11-04).** "ensure the timeline has some kind of indicator that shows where we are now in the project at any given time." Because the axis is sequence only, now is the frontier between finished and unfinished work: a Now marker across the timeline at the earliest dependency step that still has unfinished cards (in the current milestone, i.e. the first milestone with unfinished work). It carries a plain label, e.g. "Now: Phase 62, step 4 of 9 · 3 in progress, 2 ready"; finished steps behind it are subtly dimmed so progress is visible at a glance; a Jump to now control in the toolbar; the timeline opens centred on the marker; the marker moves (glides, or jumps under reduced motion) when live updates move the frontier; screen readers get "Now" announced in the ordered list and the label as text. Pure computation in timeline.ts, tested (including all done, nothing started, and work running ahead of an unfinished earlier step).

### Claude's discretion

</decisions>
