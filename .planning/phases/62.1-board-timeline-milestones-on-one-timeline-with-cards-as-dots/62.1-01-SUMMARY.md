# Phase 62.1 Plan 01: Timeline layout and host wiring Summary

**A pure buildTimeline in src/domain/team/timeline.ts places every board card as a dot at its dependency depth inside its milestone (phase dependencies as zero-weight gates, loops found by an iterative Tarjan walk and laid out in number order), and every board state the host posts now carries it, with finish dates for cards marked done and a safe fallback when the layout throws.**

## Accomplishments

- `buildTimeline(state, model, options)`: one dot per model card (plans, board items, planless phase cards); milestone bands in version order then `Added on the board`; step = longest path over every dependency inside the band, finished or not; slots in phase then plan order; edges between two cards on the board, cross-milestone ones marked and never moving a step; `waitsFor`, `missing`, `neededBy`, `inCycle` on each dot; `finished`, `foldedByDefault`, `steps`, `cycle`, `cardCount`, `cancelled` and the phase spans on each band. `now` and `percent` are declared for 62.1-02.
- Robust against hostile graphs (T-62.1-01): no recursion anywhere; 2000 cards in 40 phases lay out in well under the 500 ms bound (the whole test, model building included, took about 120 ms); a 5000-card chain with 50 eleven-card loops completes and keeps one step per card.
- Proven on this repository's roadmap at HEAD (D-03): v0.6.0 fully drawn, every in-milestone edge forward, phases 61, 62 and 62.1 after the phases they depend on, every 62.1 plan after 62-27 and 61-31, no loop, two builds equal.
- Host (`boardView.ts model()`): attaches the timeline of the same state and viewer; `finishedText` (local short date) on done cards whose history holds the mark done; a throwing layout posts no timeline and `timelineProblem: true`, the cards are unchanged, and one line goes to the NEAR AI Team output with no card text (T-62.1-02, T-62.1-03).
