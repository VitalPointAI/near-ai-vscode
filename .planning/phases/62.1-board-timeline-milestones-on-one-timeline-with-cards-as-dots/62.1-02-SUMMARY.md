# Phase 62.1 Plan 02: Timeline percent, Now and dot words Summary

**The timeline model now carries every word people and screen readers get: one rounded-down percent with its exact count on the toolbar, every milestone and every phase; the Now step at the earliest unfinished step of the first band with work, with its counts and sentence; and each dot's accessible name, preview lines, links and notes, all built from a new plain-language text table in src/domain/team/timelineText.ts.**

## Accomplishments

- **Percent (D-14):** `percentOf` counts every card that is not Cancelled and finishes every card in Done, `Math.floor(finished * 100 / counted)`. 199 of 200 is 99, planless phase cards in Backlog are unfinished, a phase the roadmap records as done is finished. `timeline.percent` is always set (`No cards yet` or `Every card here was cancelled` with no `percent` when nothing is counted). Each band gets `percent`, `heading` (`Version 1, current milestone, 50% done`), `percentText` (`50%`), `progressText` (`2 of 4 cards finished`), `tileText` (`Finished: 30 done, 2 cancelled`) and `tileName` (`Show {title}, finished, 32 cards`); each phase gets `percent`, `labels` (`Phase 1: {title} · 66%`, `Phase 1 · 66%`, `1 · 66%`) and `heading`.
- **Now (D-13):** `now` is at the earliest step holding an unfinished card in the first band with one (milestones in version order, then `Added on the board`). Work running ahead does not move it. `phaseId` is the phase of the first unfinished dot in slot order. `inProgress` counts the band's Claimed, In progress, In review and Shipping cards and `ready` its Ready cards. `title`, `detail` and `sentence` match the UI-SPEC amendment. When everything is done, `allDone` stands after the last step of the last band with cards. With no cards there is no `now`.
- **Dot words (D-05, D-10):** `label` is the card's own `a11yLabel` with the assignee said exactly once (`Assigned to you.` replaces the viewer's own name; unassigned cards add `You added this.` or `Nobody is assigned.`), then `Step {s} of {n} in {milestone}.`, `Waits for {up to 3} and {k} more.` or `Waits for nothing.`, and `Needed by {k} cards.` when k > 0. Plus `stepText`, `stepFact`, `whoText`, `waitsText`, `waitsForLinks`, `neededByLinks` and `notes` (`Waits for 61-09, which is not on the board.`, the cycle sentence). Bands get `cycleNote`, `emptyText` (`No plans yet`) and the role's `emptyNext`.
- **Hostile text (T-62.1-04):** every title, name and id goes through `flattenName` in timelineText.ts. The hostile test walks every string in the timeline: no control or format character anywhere, and no dash that a teammate did not write.
- **Repository roadmap at HEAD:** Now is in v0.6.0 or later, every earlier band and step is finished, and the Now step holds an unfinished card. The overall percent matches the cards' own count, and every dot names its step.
- Nothing signed, stored or posted changed shape except the additive timeline fields. `cardLabel` and `phaseCardLabel` output is unchanged (the board model tests pass).
