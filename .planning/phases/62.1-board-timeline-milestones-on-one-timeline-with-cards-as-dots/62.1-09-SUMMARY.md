## Deviations from Plan

### Real-window defects found and fixed (Rule 1, each with a regression test)

**1. The Now dot's preview covered the Now pill under 520 px**
- **Found during:** Task 2, the first horizontal part, in tl-h-dark-480-hover. Every theme hit it at 480.
- **Issue:** the Now dot sits between the Now pill on one side and the rail's phase labels on the other. At the full view width no side was clear, and the last fallback ignored the obstacles.
- **Fix:** with no clear side at the full width, below and above are tried from the dot's left edge to the view's right edge. `putLayer` measures again at the narrower width.
- **Commits:** 1130521 / d71f30d

**2. The card spilled 2 px below the view**
- **Found during:** Task 2, at the right edge in dark at 900.
- **Issue:** `heightOf` used `scrollHeight`, which leaves out the 1 px borders.
- **Fix:** the height now adds offsetHeight less clientHeight.
- **Commits:** 1130521 / d71f30d

**3. Done dots were too faint in the light theme**
- **Found during:** Task 2, at rest in light.
- **Issue:** 150 dot marks and glyphs were at 2.0 to 1 against the canvas (rule 9 asks 3 to 1).
- **Fix:** done uses the chart green, with the test-passed colour as the fallback.
- **Commits:** 1130521 / d71f30d

### Other auto-fixed issues

- **Commits:** 023b129 / f6bef3c

**5. [Rule 3 - Blocking] A part removed the other part's frames**

- **Commit:** afa2300

**6. [Rule 1 - Test timing] The code-server test clicked Move to... during the card's opening motion**
- The first code-server run measured the card's buttons while the card was still springing open (200 ms, scale 0.85 to 1). The click missed Move to..., and three checks failed.
- The test now waits 500 ms after the card opens, as the VS Code scenario already did. This is not a product defect: the motion is the UI-SPEC's, and a person cannot aim within it.
- **Commit:** 89a17f4
