# Phase 62.1 Plan 07: Timeline probe rules and real input Summary

**The test-only layout probe now measures the drawn timeline whenever `#root[data-view="timeline"]` is set. It checks every UI-SPEC probe rule (1 to 10) and the amendment's Now marker and percent rules (11, 12), and reports each problem as one plain sentence, for example `Dots plan 1-02 and plan 1-04 overlap by 6 px`. The DevTools client can now hover, click, turn the wheel with Ctrl and press the timeline's keys with Ctrl and Shift, using real input only.**

## Accomplishments

- **What the probe measures (collectTimeline):** the probe uses the UI-SPEC selectors to find:
  - each `button.tl-dot`, with its id, label, milestone, step, slot, state, box and mark (the main shape's colour, plus the glyph's colour on what it sits on), its `.tl-initials` chip and its `.tl-id` label
  - `.tl-more`, `.tl-folded` and `.tl-phase-label` (with how much text is cut, whether there is an ellipsis and a title, and whether a percent is shown)
  - each `.tl-ms-head` (sticky, alpha, title cut) with its `.tl-ms-percent` and `.progress-text`
  - `svg.tl-lines path[data-from][data-to]`. The line ends come from `getPointAtLength` through `getScreenCTM`. When a card is hidden, its end is attached to the `+n` button or the tile.
  - `.tl-now-line` and `.tl-now-pill` (each text leaf, line colour), `#tl-overall`, `#tl-legend`, `#tl-preview`, `#tl-card` (texts, buttons, whether the title is clamped), the focused dot, the toolbar controls' boxes, other elements that scroll, and the editor font size
- **Report:** `report.timeline` holds the fields the plan names, plus a few more: `editorFontSize`, `canvas`, `percents`, `legend`, `otherScrollers`, line labels, and per-text `texts` lists. It appears only in the Timeline view. `firstScreen` reports `nowVisible` and `nowStepDotVisible`. `metrics` is copied from `NearAiTimeline.metrics()` when that function exists.
- **The rules (checkTimeline, pure):**
  1. Overlaps of more than 1 px between dots, chips that stick out of their dot, ID labels, phase labels, `+n` buttons and headers.
  2. Anything outside the canvas, and text cut without an ellipsis and a title.
  3. The preview and the card: they stay inside the visible area less 8 px, they are not under a header that is held at the edge, and they do not cover their own dot. Also: no text line is cut, the card title is not clamped, text is at least 0.85 of the editor font, and buttons are at least 24 by 24 px.
  4. Headers, the legend, the preview and the card are opaque.
  5. The page does not scroll sideways or down, and nothing else scrolls.
  6. The focused dot is not hidden under a header that is held at the edge, and is fully visible.
  8. Each line end is within 2 px of its mark, `+n` button or tile.
  9. Marks and glyphs reach 3:1. Initials and the text in the preview and the card reach 4.5:1.
  10. Hit targets are at least 24 by 24 px and at least 4 px apart.
  11. The Now line does not cross dots or labels. The pill does not overlap dots, labels, headers, the preview or the card. The pill is opaque, and its text is not cut, is large enough and reaches 4.5:1. The line reaches 3:1.
  12. The project progress, band percents, progress texts and phase labels that show a percent are readable and stay off dots, the preview and the card.
  
  Each rule writes at most 12 sentences, then `And N more ...`.
- **Actions:** in the Timeline view, `scroll` scrolls `.tl-scroll`, and `focus` with `itemId` focuses the dot whose `data-id` matches.
- **cdp.ts:**
  - `mouseMove(cdp, to, { from, steps })` sends `mouseMoved` with button none and buttons 0.
  - `mouseClick(cdp, at)` sends a move, then a press and a release with the left button.
  - `wheel(cdp, at, { deltaX, deltaY, ctrl })` sends `mouseWheel` with modifiers 2 when ctrl is set.
  - `pressKey(cdp, key, { ctrl, shift })` sends modifiers 2, 8 or 10 on both events. It now also knows ArrowUp, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown, Tab, Space, F10, n, +, =, - and 0.
