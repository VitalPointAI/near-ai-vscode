# Phase 62.1 Plan 06: The timeline in motion Summary

**The timeline now follows the work as it changes. A teammate's change shows in place: the changed dots pulse and get the board's outline, the Now marker glides with the past shade behind it, the percents fill, and the Now sentence is said once after the change summary. The person can zoom and pan with buttons, keys, the wheel or a drag. Fit milestone fits the current milestone. The timeline opens on now and remembers the scroll for each direction. Jump to now works. Every motion in the UI-SPEC runs at its duration, stops at once when the person presses or types, and is skipped under reduced motion. Test pages can report the draw time and frame timing for the real-window budget check.**

## Accomplishments

- **Motion engine (timeline.js):**
  - `motion(node, cls, ms, { from, to, delay, done })` adds a class that the stylesheet animates.
  - With `from`, the start value is written inline (FLIP: the old place as a transform) and let go two animation frames later. No layout is read inside a frame.
  - `finishMotion()` ends every running motion at its end state. It runs from a capture listener on the scroller on pointerdown and keydown, and at the start of every zoom, Fit milestone and Jump to now.
  - `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is read from `matchMedia` at load and on every change. When it is on, no motion class is ever added, the end values are written directly, and a change while the page is open ends whatever is running.
  - `will-change: transform` is set only while a transform moves, and every animated selector has its reduced-motion rule.
- **Live updates (D-08, D-13, D-14, BOARD-02):**
  - Each draw is compared with the last one. A dot whose state changed pulses (a 2 px ring in its new state colour, 600 ms) and gets the board's static 3 second outline, which reduced motion keeps.
  - The Now line and pill glide 320 ms to their new place, and the past shade scales from the band start.
  - The toolbar ring (stroke-dashoffset), the band bars and the phase fills fill over 400 ms from the old value. The first draw shows the values at once.
  - A changed Now step is said once, after the board's batched change summary (`board.announceAfter`).
  - A milestone that finishes while it is shown stays shown for the session and starts folded on the next open. One that stops being finished is saved as shown, so it stays unfolded.
- **Zoom and pan (D-09):**
  - Zoom out, Zoom in, and `+` `=` `-` `0` with no modifier step through 50 to 200 %. Ctrl or Cmd plus a key is left to VS Code.
  - Ctrl or Cmd plus wheel zooms one level per 100 units, anchored at the pointer by the nearest dot's mark. Every level change is said ("Zoom {n}%.") and saved, and only the spacing changes.
  - In horizontal, a vertical wheel with no vertical room pans along the axis.
  - A drag on empty canvas pans after 4 px with the grabbing cursor. The click that ends a pan is swallowed. A press and release without moving closes the card. A press on a dot never pans.
- **Fit milestone:** picks the largest level at which the Now band (else the active band) fits the scroller along the axis, at least 50 %, and centres the band.
- **First screen and saved scroll (D-09, D-13):**
  - The first open of a direction centres the Now marker along the axis. With no marker it centres the active band. The scroll across the axis is 0.
  - Later opens restore the saved scroll for that direction. It is saved 250 ms after scrolling stops, and also on leaving the view and on a direction switch.
  - Coming from Columns with a card selected, that dot is centred and focused, and this wins over the Now centring.
  - In vertical, a width change draws the timeline again, so the gutter follows the 900 px rule (the 62.1-04 follow-up).
- **Jump to now and N:** the marker is centred with `scrollTo` (behavior smooth, or auto under reduced motion). Focus moves to the first unfinished dot of the Now step and the Now sentence is said. WINDOWS.md entry 20 is fixed.
- **The rest of the Motion table (D-08):**
  - On the first draw in a page, dots in or near the view appear with a delay of min(step x 24, 600) ms over 160 ms. Dots far away appear at once, and the lines fade in after the last dot.
  - Moved dots go from their old place on screen to the new one: 240 ms for a zoom, a fold, a stack or a live re-layout, and 320 ms for a direction switch, when the headers, axis, rail and marker also cross-fade over 160 ms. The lines hide while the dots move and fade back over 120 ms.
  - The View switch fades the incoming view in over 160 ms, in both directions.
  - The card springs open in 200 ms from the corner nearest its dot and closes over 120 ms. The preview fades in and slides 4 px away from its dot.
  - When the emphasis moves to another card, its lines draw in over 240 ms (24 at most, solid lines only).
- **Metrics (T-62.1-14):** `NearAiTimeline.metrics(options)` exists only when the body has `data-e2e="1"`. It returns `{ drawMs, frames: { count, p95 } }`. Frames are sampled by one animation-frame loop while a motion, a pan or a zoom runs, plus 30 frames after a pan or zoom step. The sample is capped at 600 intervals and the loop stops when nothing moves.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The fake DOM had no capture phase**
- **Found during:** Task 3 GREEN (the card's closing motion)
- **Issue:** the page ends running motions in a capture listener on the scroller. In a browser that listener runs before the card's own Escape handler. The fake ran every listener in bubble order, so the motion that Escape had just started was ended at once.
- **Fix:** `FakeNode` now keeps capture listeners (`true` or `{ capture: true }`) and runs them from the root down to the target before the target and bubble listeners, as a browser does. Listeners added without capture behave exactly as before. All 487 tests of the board, team board, timeline and sidebar-page family pass (1 skipped, a pre-existing PR #7 parity check).
- **Files modified:** src/test/helpers/fakeDom.ts
- **Commit:** 901f41b

## Next Phase Readiness (notes for 62.1-08)

- **Let the motion settle before measuring or taking a screenshot.** A probe or screenshot taken during a motion sees scaled or translated boxes:
  - After the first open, wait about 1 s: the stagger is up to 600 ms plus 160 ms, and the lines fade in over 200 ms after that.
  - After a zoom, a fold or a stack, wait at least 300 ms. After a direction switch, wait at least 400 ms.
  - After a card opens, wait at least 250 ms (scale 0.85 to 1). After it closes, wait at least 150 ms. While closing, `#tl-card` is still on the page with `aria-hidden="true"`, `inert` and `tl-card-out`, and probe rule 3 should treat it as closed.
  - The preview needs 150 ms.
  - Alternatively, run a frame set with reduced motion on: no class is ever added and everything is at its end at once.
- **`now` and `now-moved` frames:**
  - Jump to now uses `scrollTo({ behavior: 'smooth' })`, so wait for the scroll to end (no `scroll` event for about 200 ms) before the `now` frame.
  - After a live update that moves the Now step, the marker glides for 320 ms plus 2 frames, and the Now sentence arrives with the board's change summary, about 500 ms after the state.
- **Metrics:**
  - `NearAiTimeline.metrics()` returns `{ drawMs, frames: { count, p95 } }`. `metrics({ reset: true })` returns the sample, then clears it.
  - For a budget check: reset, then do the action (the stagger after a fresh open, a pan by pointer drag on empty canvas, a zoom by Zoom in or Ctrl plus wheel), wait about 1 s, then read. The real-window probe already carries `report.timeline.metrics`.
  - `drawMs` is measured from the start of the first render that has a model to the first animation frame after the first draw.
- **First screen (probe rule 7):** a fresh profile (scroll 0, 0 and no direction opened) centres the Now line. If the screenshot run reuses a profile with a saved scroll, that direction restores it instead; clear the saved webview state or switch direction to get the first screen of the other one.
- **Columns:** switching back to Columns fades the kanban or list in for 160 ms, so capture Columns frames after it.
