# Phase 62.1 Plan 04: Drawing the timeline Summary

**The Timeline view now draws the whole project from the model and the 62.1-03 geometry. Milestones are bands with opaque sticky headers (title, Current, percent, progress bar and "N of M cards finished", Fold milestone), and phases sit on a rail with labels and fill bars. Every card is a dot whose state reads by colour, shape and glyph, with initials and Mine. Faint dependency lines run under the dots. The page also draws ghosts for filters, folded tiles, +n stacks, the empty, loop, filter and error states, and the Now marker with its past shade and screen reader item. The toolbar gains the overall ring, zoom group, Jump to now and the legend. All the CSS is in one theme-only `/* timeline */` section of board.css.**

## Accomplishments

- **Bands and headers (D-02, D-14):**
  - Each `li.tl-milestone[data-milestone]` is placed at its geometry rect.
  - `div.tl-ms-head` stays in the band's flow so it can be sticky and opaque. It holds `h2.sr-only` (the model's heading), `span.tl-ms-title` (with the title in `title`), the `Current` badge, `span.tl-ms-percent`, `div.progress[role=progressbar]` (`aria-valuenow`, `aria-label "{title} progress"`) and `span.progress-text`.
  - `button.tl-fold` ("Fold milestone", `aria-label "Fold {title}"`) appears only on a finished milestone that is shown and does not hold Now.
  - The axis and step ticks run through the mark centre.
- **Phase rail:**
  - `span.tl-phase-label[aria-hidden]` shows `NearAiTimeline.pickLabel(phase.labels, room, measure)`, the longest label that fits. In the page, measure reads `scrollWidth`; the vm uses 7 px per character. The heading goes in `title`.
  - `h3.tl-phase-head.sr-only` keeps the full heading for screen readers.
  - `div.tl-phase-bar` has a `span.tl-phase-fill` scaled to the phase percent.
- **Dots (D-05):**
  - Each `button.tl-dot` has `data-state` (blocked, then held, then the column) and a shape class: hollow, dashed or filled, plus `tl-square` for a phase card.
  - Inside is `svg.tl-mark`: a circle or rounded-square body, then `g.tl-glyph` with the UI-SPEC glyph path taken from `ICONS` through `api.icon`, then `circle.tl-pip` for a plan that looks finished.
  - From 100% the dot shows `span.tl-initials`: filled (`mine`) for the viewer's own cards, with the person glyph when nobody is assigned, and hollow for others. At 50% and 75% the mark gets `tl-ring` instead. At 200% it also shows `span.tl-id`.
  - The dot has `aria-label` = `dot.label` and `aria-describedby` pointing at the hidden hint (with or without "Press M to move it" from `api.hasMenu`). There is one tab stop.
- **Lines (D-07):**
  - One `svg.tl-lines[aria-hidden]` sits under the dots, with one `path.tl-line[data-from][data-to]` (plus `waiting`) per edge.
  - A path is straight within a row and a cubic curve otherwise, with control points half a step pitch along the axis.
  - A path ends at mark edges (box x + 24 or + 4, or y + 24 or + 4 in vertical), at the `+n` edge for a hidden card, or at a folded tile's edge.
- **Filters (TD-14):** the cards that `api.matches` leaves out become `span.tl-ghost` 8 px rings in `div.tl-ghosts[aria-hidden]`, at their mark. They have no text, are not buttons, are not in the list and have no lines. Nothing matching shows the board's own empty filter state in a sticky opaque `div.tl-filter-empty` at the top of the scroller, with the ghosts below it.
- **States:**
  - `button.tl-folded` shows the title, `tileText`, the percent and "Show milestone" (`aria-label` = `tileName`, `aria-expanded=false`). Both the tile and Fold milestone save `shown`.
  - `button.tl-more[data-step]` shows "+22" ("Show 22 more cards in step 2"), and once open "Show fewer". Open stacks last for the session only.
  - `div.tl-empty` shows `emptyText` and `emptyNext`.
  - `div.banner.banner-notice.tl-cycle[role=note]` shows `cycleNote`, in a new 80 px geometry row.
  - `div.state-box.tl-error[role=alert]` shows "The timeline could not be drawn", its body, Show columns (`setView('columns')`) and Draw the timeline again (posts `{ type: 'retry' }`). It appears when there is no timeline, on `timelineProblem`, or when the layout throws.
- **Now (D-13):** `li.tl-now` goes in the named phase's `ol.tl-dots`, right before the first dot of the Now step (sorted by step, then slot). It holds `div.tl-now-line` and `div.tl-now-pill` (both `aria-hidden`; title and detail) and an `sr-only` sentence. `div.tl-past` shades in `div.tl-shades[aria-hidden]` cover every band before the frontier band and the frontier band up to the line. When everything is done, the pill reads the all-done title. With no Now, nothing is drawn.
- **Toolbar (D-14):**
  - `board.toolbarSlot()` holds four controls in order:
    - `div#tl-overall[role=progressbar]`: a 16 px ring drawn with `stroke-dashoffset`, `aria-valuenow`, `aria-label "Project progress"`, `aria-valuetext` = `percent.label`, and the visible `percent.text`.
    - `div.zoom#tl-zoom[role=group]`: out, `output.zoom-level`, in, Fit milestone; `aria-disabled` at the ends.
    - `button#tl-now` "Jump to now".
    - `button#tl-legend-btn`.
  - `div#tl-legend[role=group]` is a band in `#board` above the scroller. It holds the 15 legend items with their signs and the hint. Show legend and Hide legend toggle it, and the choice is saved through `setUiState({ legendOpen })`.
- **CSS (D-12, TD-15, TD-16):** the `/* timeline */` section of board.css uses theme variables only. It covers the state table (colour set on the mark, shapes paint with `currentColor`), the opaque header, pill, legend, tile, filter band, preview and card, and the widget shadow on `#tl-preview` and `#tl-card` only. Dots get a 2 px focus ring. High contrast adds 1 px contrast rings and borders and the contrast Now line. Forced colours use CanvasText, Canvas and Highlight, with no shade. Only transform, opacity and stroke-dashoffset transitions are declared, and reduced motion removes all of them. Layers: headers 3, card 6, preview 7, all under the menu (10).
