# Phase 62.1 Plan 05: The dots at work Summary

**A dot now opens into the board's own card, with the same face code, the same Move to... menu and the same Open details drawer. A hover or focus preview shows the card's state, title, who has it and its step. The dot's lines light up. One pure placement function keeps both layers inside the view and off the Now pill, the sticky headers and the percent labels. The whole timeline works from the keyboard in either direction, including N to jump to now, and focus never falls to the page body on a live update.**

## Accomplishments

- **Shared card face (D-06, owner rule "shared, not copied"):**
  - `updateCard` now builds its face through `cardFace(c, options)`: the state row and ID, the title, the meta row, the Backlog rule, and the pending or refusal line.
  - `moveOf(c)` holds the Move to... rule (visible, disabled, reason).
  - The kanban DOM is unchanged, and boardClient, boardMenu, boardDetail and boardDrag pass unchanged.
- **New board api:** `cardFace`, `select`, `pendingText` and `refusalText`.
  - `focusCard` hands off to the timeline's `focus(id)`, so the menu and the drawer give focus back to the dot.
  - `refreshCard` calls the timeline's `refresh`, so pending and refusal changes draw on the dot and in the card.
- **Expanded card:** `section#tl-card[role=dialog][aria-modal=false][aria-labelledby=tl-card-title]` sits in `.tl-canvas` after the dots.
  - It has `h2#tl-card-title[tabindex=-1]`, which takes focus and holds the unclamped `span.card-title-full`, and a Close card icon button.
  - Below that come the board face and the facts `dl`: Step, Waits for (link buttons, or Nothing), Needed by (left out at 0), and Finished (only for a done card with `finishedText`).
  - Then the notes (the missing dependency and loop notes), the board's pending or refusal line with its Dismiss button, and the actions row: `button.secondary.move-btn` "Move to..." (hidden, or disabled with the board's reason) and "Open details".
  - The dot gets `selected`, `aria-expanded="true"` and `aria-controls="tl-card"`. A pending dot gets `pending` and `aria-busy`. A refused dot gets `refused` and `circle.tl-pip.tl-pip-error`, which replaces the info pip.
  - These ways close the card: Escape, Close card, Open details (which opens the drawer with the dot as opener), a click outside the card and the menu, and opening another dot (which switches the card).
  - If the card's card leaves the board, the card closes and the page announces "That card is no longer on the board."
- **Placement:** `NearAiTimeline.place(anchor, size, view, obstacles, mode)` returns `{ left, top, width, height, side, capped }`.
  - The page passes the dot box, a view equal to the scroller less 8 px, and these obstacles: the Now pill, the stuck headers (+8 px) and the phase labels.
  - The card is placed again only when its dot moves, the scroller changes size, or the zoom or direction changes. A ResizeObserver handles the size change. Panning keeps it beside its dot.
- **Preview:** `div#tl-preview[aria-hidden=true]`.
  - It holds the board face's state row and ID, the title (3-line clamp), the face's badges, whoText (for example "Nobody yet"), stepText and waitsText, and "Click to open the card." or "Press Enter to open the card."
  - It opens 300 ms after the pointer enters a dot, or at once on focus. It closes 100 ms after the pointer leaves, and at once on blur, on Escape and when the card opens.
  - Within 600 ms of a close, the next dot's preview opens with no delay.
- **Emphasis (D-07):** the hovered dot's in and out paths get `emphasis`. If no dot is hovered, the focused dot's do, and if none is focused, the open card's do. `waiting` stays from the model. Emphasis is applied again after every draw.
- **Keyboard (D-10, D-13, TD-19):**
  - There is one roving tab stop over the dots, tiles and +n buttons, and it is kept across redraws.
  - Along the axis, keys move to the next step and keep the slot, else take the last slot. Across the axis, keys move within the step.
  - Home and End go to the first and last stop of the milestone, and Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End to those of the whole timeline. Page Down and Page Up go to the first stop of the next or previous phase, and a tile counts as one phase.
  - Enter on +n shows the whole stack and keeps the slot. Show fewer goes back to +n.
  - M, Shift+F10 and ContextMenu open Move to... anchored to the dot.
  - Escape closes the menu first, then the card, then the preview.
  - N moves focus to the first unfinished dot of the Now step and announces the Now sentence.
  - Keys with any other modifier, Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- among them, are left to VS Code.
  - `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest', inline: 'nearest' })` brings the focused stop into view, with a 48 px `scroll-margin` from the CSS.
- **Plain language:**
  - "Nobody yet" comes from the model's `whoText` (`WHO_NOBODY` in `src/domain/team/timelineText.ts`) and shows in the preview.
  - "No estimate" comes from the board face's estimate (`estimateText` in `src/domain/team/boardText.ts`) and shows in the expanded card. Both are covered by tests.
  - The new page words are in timeline.js's `T` table, which the plain-language scan reads. "Press N to jump to now" is a single literal.

## Next Phase Readiness (notes for 62.1-06)

- **Jump to now:** `#tl-now` still has no handler (WINDOWS 20). `jumpToNow()` in timeline.js already focuses the right stop and announces the Now sentence. 06 should call it from the button and add the centring scroll (smooth, or `auto` under reduced motion) in place of the `scrollIntoView` nearest.
- **Re-placing the layers:** `placeCard(box)` places the card again only when its key changes (box, direction, zoom, scroller size). `putLayer` measures `scrollHeight`.
  - After a FLIP or zoom, call the draw (it follows the card and the preview), or clear `open.at` and call `placeCard`.
  - The motion for "springs open" and "closes" can wrap `showLayer`. The card is attached in `showLayer(cardBox, true)` and removed in `showLayer(cardBox, false)`, so a closing animation needs to delay that removal.
- **Lines:** emphasis is applied by `applyEmphasis()` at the end of every draw and on hover, focus, open and close. Draw-in on emphasis (stroke-dashoffset) should hook there, with at most 24 paths.
- **Keyboard zoom keys** (`+ = - 0`, no modifier) are not taken yet. The keydown handler on `list` returns early for them (navTarget gives undefined), so 06 can add them there or on the scroller.
- **Focus survival:** `render` records the focused stop, or the open card's dot, before the draw, and `keepFocus` puts focus back after it. A FLIP must keep the stop nodes (it does: dots are kept by id).
- **Selection across views:** a card opened in Timeline is `ui.selected`, and the draw's tab stop prefers the selected dot. Centring the selected dot on open is 06's job.
- **For 08 (real window):** with the edge-case dots, probe rule 3 should find `#tl-card` and `#tl-preview` only while they are shown. `data-side` on both says which side was chosen.
