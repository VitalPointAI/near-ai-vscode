# Phase 62.1 Plan 03: Timeline view, switches and geometry Summary

**The board now has a View switch (Columns, Timeline) first in toolbar row 2 and a Direction switch saved at state version 3. Under 520 px the timeline is always drawn top to bottom. A new timeline.js registers the view and draws the screen reader lists from model.timeline. It also exports a pure geometry function that places every band, phase label, dot, +n box, tile and the Now marker, and the tests prove nothing touches at every zoom, direction and width.**

## Accomplishments

- **View switch (D-04, TD-2):** `div.segmented#view-switch[role=radiogroup][aria-label=View]` is the first control of row 2 in the kanban and list layouts, in both views. It has the same arrow keys as Group by. Switching says `Showing the timeline.` or `Showing columns.`, keeps the selected card, and sets `#root[data-view]`. In Timeline, `renderMain` hands the shown cards to the registered view and puts nothing else into `#board`.
- **timeline.js:** one strict IIFE that reads `window.NearAiBoard`. It draws `div.tl-scroll[role=region]` > `ol.tl-milestones` > `li.tl-milestone[data-milestone]` (with `h2` = `milestone.heading`) > `ol.tl-phases` > `li.tl-phase[data-phase]` > `h3.tl-phase-label` (= `phase.heading`) + `ol.tl-dots` > `li` > `button.tl-dot[data-id][data-step][data-slot][data-state][aria-label=dot.label]`. Dots without a phase of their band (the board band) go in one `li.tl-phase[data-phase="none"]` named after the band. Nodes are reused across renders, so focus survives. There is one tab stop: the selected card's dot, else the first. The selected dot has `aria-current`. Nothing is draggable and nothing is posted.
- **Saved choices v3 (TD-18, T-62.1-08):** `UI_VERSION = 3`. A version 2 state keeps every field and gains `view: 'columns'` and the timeline defaults. An unversioned state follows the 62-21 rule. `timeline { direction, zoom, legendOpen, shown, scroll }` is checked value by value. Unsafe keys (`__proto__`, `constructor`, `prototype`) and non-boolean `shown` entries are dropped, `shown` is capped at 500 entries, and scroll values must be finite and 0 or more.
- **Direction switch and narrow widths (TD-11):** `#direction-switch` shows only in Timeline, right after the View switch, followed by `api.toolbarSlot()` (`div.tl-tools`). In Timeline, the Milestone choice, its summary and Group by are hidden but stay in the DOM. Under 520 px the drawn direction is vertical and Horizontal has `aria-disabled="true"` and the title `Horizontal needs a wider window.`. The saved choice is unchanged and comes back when the window is wider. `The window is narrow, so the timeline is shown top to bottom.` is said once each time the narrow drawing starts.
- **Geometry (D-13, UI-SPEC tables):** `NearAiTimeline.geometry(timeline, { direction, zoom, width, shown, openStacks })` returns `{ width, height, direction, zoom, box, pitch, bands[], dots[], more[], tiles[], now? }`.
  - Each band has `header`, `phases[{ id, row, label, bar }]`, `axis` and `ticks`. Horizontal bands add `nowRow`; vertical bands add `gutter`, and the frontier band adds `nowGap`. Empty bands have `emptyBox`.
  - Dot box sizes and pitches come exactly from the tables. Overlapping phase spans are packed greedily into the fewest rail rows or bar columns. Stacks show 8 dots and then a `+n` box, or all the dots and Show fewer when the stack is open. A folded tile is 160 px wide (horizontal) or 56 px tall (vertical). An empty band is 160 px wide or has a 56 px line.
  - Horizontal has a 48 px Now row and a 24 px rail row per row. Vertical has a 56 px Now gap and a gutter of 64, 120 or 160 px. Bands are 48 px apart, and the canvas keeps 32 px of padding at the far edges.

## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions`. Where the UI-SPEC is silent I chose these values (Claude's discretion):
- Horizontal folded tile 160 x 112 px. Horizontal empty band text box 128 x 96 px.
- 16 px band padding across. The 16 px phase label line sits 2 px above its 4 px bar inside a 24 px rail row.
- 8 px between the vertical gutter and slot 0. Bar columns sit nearest the axis first, with an 8 px pitch. A vertical label that has no free line inside its segment is returned with `hidden: true` and a zero size.
- The vertical gutter grows past 64, 120 or 160 px only when its bar columns would leave labels less than 24 px wide.
- A vertical folded tile and empty line span from 16 px to 32 px short of the canvas edge.
- The horizontal Now line for step 0 is 12 px into the band (half the padding). The vertical Now gap for step 0 splits the 24 px padding.
- Phases are packed in first-step-then-number order (this is the model's order, and first fit in start order gives the fewest rows).
- `api.setUiState(patch)` re-renders and announces only when the drawn direction changes. Other fields are saved, and the view calls `api.redraw()` when it needs a new drawing.
