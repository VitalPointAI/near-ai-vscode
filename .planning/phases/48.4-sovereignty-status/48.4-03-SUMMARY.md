# Phase 48.4 Plan 03: Operator jurisdiction and location per exchange Summary

Every exchange now states whose law governs the operator of its far end and where that end is; `checkExchanges` refuses a missing field or a country name with a plain line naming the exchange, the schema requires both, and the generated interconnections table shows them as "Operator under" and "Far end" in plain words.

## What was done (8617d5a2)

- **Task 1 (tracer).** `operatorJurisdiction` (`^([A-Z]{2}|org|unknown)$`) and `location` (`^([A-Z]{2}|site|unknown)$`), required in the schema and checked in `checkExchanges` (the docs job's `hosted-docs --check` runs it). `renderTable` gains two columns after "From and to"; `org` reads "your organization", `site` "at the site", a code as written. Tests "exchange jurisdiction" (fixture rows for US/unknown, org/site, unknown/CA; every real row filled) and "refused" (missing fields, "Canada", `org` as a location).
- **Task 2.** The fifteen exchanges filled, the register's note and the document's lead sentence say what the two columns mean, and the block regenerated with `node compliance/src/cli.ts hosted-docs --write` (never hand-edited).
