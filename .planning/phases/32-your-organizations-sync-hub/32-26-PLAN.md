<objective>
Prove success criterion 3 (MUS-08): a hub that withholds, delays, replays, reorders, rolls back, forks its view, injects, corrupts a blob, refuses selectively or serves another project's stream is either caught and named by the client or does no harm; and an honest restore from an old backup is refused as a rollback and refilled by the clients.

Why a listener wrapper and not a network proxy: the session answer is bound to the TLS connection's keying material (D-35), so a proxy that ends TLS and opens a new connection to the hub would be refused by the hub. A hostile operator controls the hub process itself, so the layer sits inside it, where the client's TLS connection still ends.
