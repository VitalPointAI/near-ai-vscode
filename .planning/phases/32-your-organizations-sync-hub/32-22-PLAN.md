<objective>
Signed checkpoints (D-45, D-46): the hub fixes the final order of what it can read and signs it, so clients can tell a rolled-back or forked hub from an honest one.

Why the coverage rule: a version 2 head names the head entry's lamport and the newest roster it cites, and the order tip sorts by lamport; all of these are inside sealed bodies. The hub opens only the base cell and the cells granted to it, so it checkpoints exactly those, and only a causally closed set of them, so a client's recomputation matches. For every other cell, rollback and withholding are not caught by checkpoints: clients catch them with their high-water marks (the heads the 32-16 witness keeps per hub, used by 32-23) and with deps that cite entries never delivered. This is flagged to the owner in the phase report (D-45 and D-54 assumed full coverage).

Purpose: success criterion 2 (signs checkpoints) and the base for criterion 3 (rollback and fork detection, 32-26).
