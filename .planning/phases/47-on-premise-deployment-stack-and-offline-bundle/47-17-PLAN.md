New `recovery-plan.md` (CP-2), in plain words:
- **Purpose and roles.** Site administrator, key custodians, your organization's contingency lead.
- **The recovery order**, with the group ids from 47-27:
  - stage 0 (`platform`): nodes, RKE2 and the registry rebuilt from the bundle;
  - 1 `secrets`: the HSM and OpenBao;
  - 2 `identity`: Keycloak and Postgres;
  - 3 `registry`: the registry's service; admission back to Deny;
  - the `audit-pipeline` gate: OpenSearch and Fluent Bit running before anyone is let back in;
  - 4 `sessions`; 5 `hubs`; 6 `audit`: historical audit data.
- **RTO and RPO.** `recovery.rtoMinutes: 480` (8 hours) and `recovery.rpoMinutes: 1440` (24 hours), accepted by the owner (decision c). They are the single source 48.1's system description reads. Run `drill.mjs plan` to see each schedule against the RPO.
- **Quarterly restore drill from the off-site copy (owner decision d).** Record each step with `drill.mjs record --add-step`:
  1. `offsite.mjs verify --day <recent day> --sample <n>`;
  2. a Longhorn restore of one volume backup from the copy at the second site;
  3. the archive verifier on the copied archive.
- **Degraded modes.**
  - HQ lost: edges keep running on their own Keycloak, OpenBao and hubs. No new bundles, user moves or intermediates arrive until HQ is back. The HQ root's revocation list must stay valid meanwhile (`key-ceremony.md`). HQ itself recovers in the order above.
  - An edge cut off from HQ: normal for an air-gapped edge. A linked edge that brokers HQ sign-in keeps its local people signing in, while brokered people cannot until the link returns, unless they were imported by signed user export.
- **The restore-test record** `sterling-restore-test/1`, field by field, as 47-27 defines it.
- **The drill.**
  - `drill.mjs verify --components postgres,registry-input,escrow,opensearch,volume --confirm <site name>` restores into scratch copies only.
  - The lab-only and human steps (etcd cluster reset, OpenBao snapshot restore, HSM checks) are added with `drill.mjs record --add-step`.
