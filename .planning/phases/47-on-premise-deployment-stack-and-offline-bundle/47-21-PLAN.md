Write `deploy/runbooks/guest-accounts.md` (D-58 to D-64):

1. **What a guest account is for.** People from outside your organization (industry, allied military, others) who observe shared work and give feedback (Phase 33.1 Observer positions). It covers:
   - who does what: the lead asks; the security contact approves allied-military and industry guests; the site operator creates and ends the account; two key custodians approve any removal;
   - what a guest can never do: hold an administrator role, decide, approve or post anyone. The observer position limits what they do in a shared project.
2. **The states of a guest account,** in one table, each with what moves it on:
   - requested;
   - approved (allied military and industry only);
   - created (the registration link is out; fewer than two keys; can sign in but cannot sign);
   - enrolled (two keys, then `enrol.mjs enrol`);
   - active;
   - overdue (past the end date while the job has not yet run; shown by `guests.mjs list`);
   - ended (automatically or by `guests.mjs end`; records kept);
   - removed (only by the removal step).
   Say that no state waits forever. A guest who never enrols is still ended at their end date.
3. **Turning guests on at a site.** Set `guests.maxDays` and `guests.checkMinutes` in the realm site file and re-apply the realm with `deploy/keycloak/apply.mjs`. The administrator adds the security contacts to the group `security-contacts`.
4. **Creating a guest.** Give the full `guests.mjs create` command with every flag. Explain:
   - the end date as a UTC instant;
   - nationality and organization as the same tokens your organization's labels use;
   - filing the security contact's written approval under its reference.
   Then explain enrolment:
   - in person by default: the registration link goes to a site-issued guest address read at the enrolment desk, after an identity check against the visit request;
   - two keys;
   - at an air-gapped site, use site-issued security keys or a passkey on the same device. The cross-device QR flow needs an internet relay (D-57);
   - the registration link: `create` sends it through the realm's mail settings (the realm site file's `smtp`). With `--no-link`, or when delivery fails, the account stays and `guests.mjs link` sends it again; what each message means and what to check (the site mail server) first;
   - then run `enrol.mjs enrol`.
5. **Extending, and ending early.** `guests.mjs extend`, with the same approvals; it re-enables an ended guest. `guests.mjs end`, with a reason.
6. **The automatic end.** The CronJob `sterling-guest-expiry` in `sterling-identity`:
   - what it does: account disabled, sessions ended, entity disabled, Theia session stopped. The workspace volume and records are kept;
   - how to see its runs and failures (`kubectl get cronjob,jobs -n sterling-identity`, and the job's log);
   - that `guests.mjs list` shows anyone overdue;
   - that `guests.mjs expire` runs the same end by hand.
7. **A guest at another site.** `users.mjs export --include-guests` at the source and `users.mjs import --include-guests` at the destination, with that site's lead and security contact. Then `enrol.mjs enrol` there. The guest's keys never move.
8. **Collecting site-issued security keys** at the end of a visit.
9. **Removing a guest's records** after your organization's retention period. The approval step comes first:
   - written approval of the security contact and the site administrator;
   - in OpenBao, two key custodians approve each request through the Control Group: `deletion_allowed` on the two Transit keys (the `key-move` policy), then the deletes in `guest-removal`. Before approving each request, the custodians check that the entity id in its path belongs to an entity named `guest-<id>`. OpenBao cannot limit the KV and Transit delete paths to guests, so this check is the guard (T-47-76);
   - then delete the Keycloak account and, with a cluster administrator, the guest's Workspace and volume.
   Say that the guest's signed comments in a shared project's log stay. That log cannot be edited.

In `deploy/runbooks/README.md`, add one line for the new runbook, in the order a site uses it (after enrolment).
