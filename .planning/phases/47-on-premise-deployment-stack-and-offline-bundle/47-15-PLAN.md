| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-47-50 | Spoofing | oauth2-proxy to Keycloak without TLS verification | high | mitigate | Patch turns verification on with the site CA; asserted by render test |
| T-47-51 | Information disclosure | OIDC secrets in a ConfigMap | high | mitigate | Patch removes plaintext secrets; files from Secret mounted only into the sidecar |
| T-47-52 | Elevation of privilege | operator or service RBAC | medium | mitigate | Roles reviewed; narrowing applied where values allow; upstream grants recorded |
| T-47-53 | Denial of service | sessions waiting forever | low | mitigate | Launch timeout set; failures carry a reason |
| T-47-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | accept | No package installed; chart patched reproducibly with a pinned hash |
</threat_model>
