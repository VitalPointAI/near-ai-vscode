<objective>
Wire the decision algebra (30-07) into the version reducer (30-08) so that every decision on a version is derived from real signed entries: approve, reject, release, supersede and withdraw per DESIGN 15.4, contested and pending-confirmation per 15.5, every forbidden act refused, plus the per-version attribution summary, the dependants' "source changed" and the working text state (MUS-03, and MUS-02's attribution summary; ROADMAP Phase 30 success criterion 4).

Purpose: "no agent actor can approve, reject, release or supersede" and "only a fresh decision that has seen them settles it" are the owner's rules for multi-user staff decisions; this plan proves them end to end.
Output: versions.ts extended and two test files.

Runs in wave 4 because it needs decisions.ts (30-07), versions.ts (30-08), attribution.ts and attributionAtSnapshot (30-06), and checkpoints (30-09). It owns versions.ts and its two test files; 30-11 runs beside it on disjoint files.

Recorded planning choices:
- A decision act counts as unconfirmed when `roleWithdrawnBy(member, role, cited roster)` names a roster entry that is in the set and is concurrent with the decision; if the decision is in that entry's past it stands; if the entry is in the decision's past the chain already rejected it stale-roster-citation (research assumption A10, causal rather than clock-based).
- The person presented to `transition()` for an effective decision is built from the roster (`memberName`, the decision role, the device fingerprint) of the first maximal act by entry hash; all names stay in the decision output.
- Supersede is an explicit `version.supersede` act by a role holder (DESIGN 4.3 and 6.3), valid only when the newer version is released and numbered higher.
- The working text is withdrawn exactly when the newest version is withdrawn, and open again when a newer version is proposed (the smallest reading of D-23 on release.ts's states).
</objective>
