## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| admitted member -> Yjs parser | update bytes inside a validly signed, sealed entry from a possibly hostile member |
| compaction writer -> joiner | a full state blob written by any member |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-30-23 | Spoofing | text inserted under another writer's client id | high | mitigate | ownership check on every inserted struct; foreign-client rejects the whole update (Tasks 1 and 2) |
