# Phase 74.1 Plan 13: The empty-state card and the report-only notice Summary

When a request is refused by `route.passport` and no model would pass, the chat shows the card in place of the reply: "No approved model can do this", what this chat is (use and label) and where, the ways forward that hold (what NEAR AI has been asked, as a disclosure; Open Research Workspace), and who to ask last. In report mode, the first would-refuse of `route.passport` in a session raises "Agent work is running outside your organization's model policy (report only)." ("your rules for models" with no organization layer) at the top of the chat for every tab and in the status bar as "$(warning) Policy: models report only"; it has no dismiss and goes only when the rules turn to enforce. On a personal layer it offers Adopt the Western defaults, which opens a proposal in the policy editor and writes nothing.
