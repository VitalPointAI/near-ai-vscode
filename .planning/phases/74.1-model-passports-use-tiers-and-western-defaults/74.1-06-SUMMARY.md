# Phase 74.1 Plan 06: Passport lifecycle, triggers and your organization's evaluation Summary

An approval now ends when its date passes, when your organization revokes it (`all uses`), or when the model behind it changes place: the first place seen for a passport hash is kept in global storage, a change fires `hosting-change` once (one `route.passport-triggered` record) and the approval stays ended until a new signed passport. Under enforce no model takes any role above research without your organization's evaluation, except chat you review allowed by a t1-risk exception, whose assessment reference is recorded.

## What changed

- **Task 1 (aab82599):** pure `passportState` and `passportEvaluated`.
- **Tasks 2 and 3 (851152e9, one commit: step 7 and the trigger facts share passportStep.ts and routing.ts):**
  - `passportTriggers.ts`: the log, one JSON file per install, the module-level log the route check reads, set at activation in `registerGate`.
  - `routeFactsFor` (privateRoute.ts) widens its `info` pick to `forwardedTo`, resolves the passport through the current route's `routePolicy` models, records the first place, fires a listed trigger once and writes `passportTriggeredRecordFor` (sessionMark.ts) through the route's sink, and passes `observed` with the passport hash. `nearAiClient.ts` unchanged (1,860 lines).
  - `passportStep`: `passportState` before the tier check (revoked at every use; a fired trigger at every use above research, gap `expired` with the trigger's words); then `passportCovers`; then, above research, `passportEvaluated` unless the t1-risk exception path at T1. Observations are read only for the request's own passport hash.
  - `PASSPORT_GAP_TEXT.expired` names the trigger ("ended because the place it runs changed"); `TRIGGER_WORDS`, `passportTriggeredText`.
