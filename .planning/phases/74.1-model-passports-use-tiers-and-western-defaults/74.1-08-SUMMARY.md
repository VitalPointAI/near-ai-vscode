# Phase 74.1 Plan 08: The different-family rule and the run's models by job Summary

A checker is never of the family of the model it checks: the safety classifier of a gpt-oss run is Nemotron 3 Nano, then Granite Guardian; of a Nemotron run, gpt-oss; with neither allowed, a person decides and the notice says why. The family comes only from the passport (a Nemotron post-trained on Llama is a Llama). Every run starts on the tiers `jobModelsFor` resolves with the gate's own preview, and the classifier's request runs as a checking job, so under enforce an unevaluated classifier is refused before anything is sent.

- **Task 1 (b1209f76):** `lineage.ts` (pure) and `gateAllows` (decideRoute at `JOB_MINIMUM_TIER[job]`, the run's mark and policy, `checkingJob` for safety).
