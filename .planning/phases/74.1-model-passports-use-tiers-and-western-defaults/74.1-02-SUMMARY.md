# Phase 74.1 Plan 02: The enforcement tracer Summary

Under a policy whose `models` section says `mode: enforce`, an agent request for a PRC-origin model is refused by the client's existing route check before the fake fetch sees anything, with `route.passport` and the gap's own sentence; the same request for gpt-oss goes; under `report`, or with no `models` section, it goes and is recorded as would-refuse with the gap. The use comes from the session route, only rises, and every model request's record names the use, the passport and its hash, and the gap.

## What changed

- `src/domain/gate/passportStep.ts` (new, pure): the passport step moved out of `routing.ts`; `PASSPORT_GAPS` (the ten D-11 values, permanent once written), `PASSPORT_GAP_TEXT` (one sentence per gap in the refusal form, ending "Who to ask: your organization's security contact."), `SHIPPED_REPORT_MODELS`, and `authorizationRecord` (moved here so routing.ts imports it one way).
- `routing.ts`: `route.passport` moved from `ROUTE_RULES_NAMED_AHEAD` into `ROUTE_RULES` with its generic sentence; step 7 raises the use by the policy's own tables (`useTierFor(facts.useInput, models)`), refuses on a gap under enforce, reports under report, and puts `useTier`, `passport { id, hash }` and `passportGap` on the decision. `RouteFacts.useInput` added. 989 lines (limit 1,200).
- `sessionMark.ts`: `SessionRoute.useOf`, `sessionRouteFrom(..., { use })`, `raiseSessionUse` and `sessionUseOf` (the high-water, for 74.1-03's sub-agents); `routeRecordFor` writes the new record fields.
- `privateRoute.ts`: `routeFactsFor` sets `use` and `useInput` from `currentSessionRoute()` (its facts plus its mark, shipped tables), raised by the route's high-water; T1 outside every route.
- `record.ts`: optional `useTier`, `passport`, `passportHash`, `passportGap` under `unmapped.nearAi`, record version 1, each checked against its closed list or shape. `trust.ts`: `TrustFacts.passportHash`, and the use the step checked.
- `nearAiClient.ts` is unchanged (it already calls `routeFactsFor` and `decideRoute`).
