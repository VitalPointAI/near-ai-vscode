<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A local Western model proposes labels with their bases as people write; the originator affirms the label in one signed step when they finalize, submit or release; only a person holding a `ClassificationAuthorityCredential` lowers it. The product never says the AI classified something: it proposed, and a named person affirmed.

In this phase:
- **The runner:** classification requests only to model servers your organization's policy lists, at the system-high of the content's security domain, with passport, use tier, family and second-family checks; closed JSON, no tools; failures leave the item `unassessed`, never lower (74.3-01, 74.3-03).
- **Models:** the candidate roster of owner decision D7, a licence check and a compliance fit entry for each (74.3-02).
- **Evaluation:** the four evaluation sets, per-level metrics, calibration, the signed evaluation report the passport cites, the red-team release gate (74.3-04); the blind audit, the shadow period (owner decision D10) and the rule that a model gets a role at a level only after passing there (74.3-11, 74.3-12).
- **The native Classification panel** beside the editor, its state machine, the Raise dialog and the status bar entry (74.3-05), built to the approved `74.3-UI-SPEC.md`; the **Classification Reviews** page with its four sections (74.3-09 to 74.3-12); the render and accessibility checks on the built image (74.3-15).
- **Signed affirmation** (owner decision D8) (74.3-06).
- **Agent outputs and chats:** every output an agent writes, sends or summarizes is classified and raises the chat's label (74.3-07).
- **Lowering only through `ClassificationAuthorityCredential`** (71-03, 71-06), with the request and approval cards (74.3-08, 74.3-09).
- **The spill alert** (owner decision D12) (74.3-10).
- **Cells:** a raise narrows the cell marking and drops holders at the next key; a lowering goes through `marking.approve` (74.3-13).
- **Sizing** for an HQ of about 400 people, as estimates; docs, compliance mapping and the integration PR (74.3-14).

Not in this phase:
- The engine itself (Phase 70.1). Passport enforcement, use tiers and the family rule (Phase 74.1). The credential types, checker, wallet and issuance (Phase 71 core). Cell markings and `marking.approve` themselves (Phase 33.1).
- Ingested data, the steward's weekly batch, retention and privacy facts (Phase 93), a trained classification specialist (Phase 98), the drift cards of the watching agent (Phase 99).
- Running or shipping a model server or model weights. Your organization runs its servers and brings weights in by its approved transfer; the product only calls servers the signed policy lists.

### The runner (owner decision D6 default)

- **D-03 (system-high, policy-listed servers):** The runner sends content only to an endpoint in the signed policy's `endpoints[]` whose class may hold the section's `systemHigh` (`classMayHold`) and whose ceiling allows it (`endpointCeilingAllows`), through the gate's own route decision (`decideRoute` with the system-high mark and the checking job), with a route record for every request. Never a commercial cloud: `closed-api` and `forwarded` are refused for classification whatever the label; `vendor-region` (Command A+ through Model Vault) only where the policy's routing already allows the system-high there; `org-tee` only where the policy allows that level. The runner never scans or listens for servers (46 D-06). A prediction above system-high is the engine's possible-spill outcome (70.1 D-13).
- **D-04 (passport, tier, family):** The classifier is the checking job `safety` (`JOB_MINIMUM_TIER.safety` = T2); T3 for staff projects and classified levels, which needs a passport with an evaluation reference. The passport must cover the exact artifact hash on `local`, `org-plain` and `org-tee`. Origin must be Western: a PRC-origin or `XX` passport is refused for classification at every tier. The classifier's family must differ from the family of the model that wrote the content (MPRV-09). At levels whose threshold entry has `secondFamily: true`, a second family must also find nothing to raise before the result is `none` or `kept`; disagreement takes the higher answer. All of this is 74.1's, imported and never re-implemented here (plan-check W4): the passport step of `decideRoute` and `passportGap` (I-1), `useTierFor` with `checkingJob` (I-2), `resolvePassport` as the only matcher (I-3), `familyOf` and `checkerFor` (I-4), `passportEvaluated` (I-5), `passportState` and the trigger log (I-6), the artifact rule (I-7); 74.3 adds only the classification rules (system-high, never closed-api or forwarded, a role at the level, a second family at marked levels).
- **D-05 (request and answer):** A fixed prompt template (its hash recorded) and a closed JSON answer: per scale the proposed level, compartments, releasability and handling from the policy's own vocabulary, per-level probabilities, typed facts (`{ type, subject }`, fact types from the policy), and a rationale of at most 600 characters. No tools. Content goes in a delimited data block, visible text and hidden parts separate. The policy, thresholds, system-high and code words are never in the prompt. Guide rule descriptions are included only where the endpoint may hold their label (owner decision D11 default). An answer with any other key, a person-attribute key, a tool call or text outside the JSON is refused.
- **D-06 (confidence):** Token probabilities when the server returns them under constrained output (llama.cpp's OpenAI-compatible server; 74.3-01 records the verdict from its documentation), otherwise agreement between samples and between families. One sample normally; three only when a level sits within a band edge. The model's own stated confidence is never used. Calibration per level is fitted on audited data (74.3-04) and named by id in every record.
- **D-07 (fail closed):** A timeout, refusal, unreadable answer, no allowed endpoint or a passport that does not cover the request gives `unassessed`: rules still apply (70.1 `assess` with no judgement) and the label never goes down.
- **D-08 (load and sizing, research 4.5):** Classification runs after edits settle (default 3 seconds after the last save), never per keystroke, never blocking typing. Each endpoint has bounded parallel slots (default 2); a newer revision of an item replaces its queued request. The phase's sizing record (`74.3-RESEARCH.md`, written in 74.3-14) gives the research's estimates for an HQ of about 400 people (about 30 classifiable items per person per day, 12,000 items and about 6,000 model calls a day; about 4 seconds per call on one Strix Halo host, about a second or less on one data-centre GPU; proposals 5 to 15 seconds after save at peak on Strix Halo) and recommends two inference hosts per HQ security domain, every figure marked as an estimate until Phase 85 measures it. The operator guide explains how load arises, per item and per call, in words and constant names only, with no capacity, throughput or timing number, because capacity figures come only from load runs and the docs check refuses any other (Phase 48 D-09). The estimates are also an open condition in `compliance/gaps.json` until Phase 85 measures them (74.3-14).

That suggestion is out of this phase's scope (no plan builds it); the boundary is enforced here by the schema and a test.

### The panel and the states (research 6.5)

- **D-12 (state machine):** A pure state machine in `src/domain/classify/itemState.ts`: `draft` (the inherited join), `assessing`, `proposed` (only for the current content hash), `unassessed` (no classifier, offline, timeout), `affirmed` (signed by the originator), `lower-requested` (unchanged label, stays high). `assessing` always ends in `proposed` or `unassessed`; finalize works from both and from `lower-requested` at the current label (UI-SPEC Q6); editing an affirmed item returns it to `draft` with the label kept; `unassessed` items are re-queued whenever a listed endpoint becomes reachable, and the result only raises (owner decision D9). From the UI-SPEC planner notes: an edit from `lower-requested` returns to `draft` and voids the request (P3); one `raise` event (a person's raise or an accepted suggestion) is allowed in every state except while frozen, keeps `draft`, `proposed` and `unassessed`, and moves `affirmed` and `lower-requested` to `proposed`, voiding the affirmation or the request (P12); request-lower is allowed from `affirmed` (P13); an item affirmed with no assistant goes from `affirmed` to `assessing` when an endpoint returns, and a raising result voids the affirmation while a non-raising one restores it (P14). `visibleState` adds overlays: Possible spill over every state while the item's stored freeze is set (P15), and Out of date from the active editor's Theia `Saveable` dirty flag, read in the frontend with no RPC field (P16). No state waits on the network or a model.
- **D-13 (the native panel and Classification Reviews):** The owner chose a full UI design pass (no waiver); `74.3-UI-SPEC.md` is approved (2026-11-24, owner UI decisions Q1 to Q13: "Trial" for shadow, no new rail icon, no rationale on screen, one-step own lowering, the original theme with lifted cards and more) and is the contract for every surface: the Theia view `sterling-classification` (right side, tab icon `codicon-tag`) in the 46 D-15 pattern (a `common/` interface, a backend `RpcConnectionHandler`, a frontend proxy; refusals cross the RPC as plain data), the Affirm, Raise and Request dialogs, the status bar label entry, notifications, and the main-area page Classification Reviews with its Requests, Possible spills, Model evaluation and Blind audit sections. Engine code reaches the workbench only through `workbench/tools/classify-engine-entry.ts` in `build-engine.mjs`. Every sentence is the spec's copy key as a `*_TEXT` constant; `{label}` appears only on the item's own surfaces and `{level label}` everywhere else; every button is a verb plus a noun; the trust line is "Proposed with {model} · Runs on {place}" with the model approval id only under Show trust details (P1, Q9); colours only from Theia variables and the spec's elevation tokens. Workbench tests sit flat in `workbench/test/` (its runner globs `dist/test/*.test.js`). Words are checked against GLOSSARY (coherence review gate) and the spec's render and contrast checks run on the built image in 74.3-15.
- **D-14 (where the rationale lives):** The rationale and fact subjects are derived content at the item's label. They stay with the item: in the item's cell for staff products, and in the workbench's per-workspace store under `.near-ai/state/classify/` for files (the floor already keeps agents out of `.near-ai/state`). Only the record's log copy (hashes, ids, kinds) goes to the decision log.

### Affirmation (owner decision D8 default)

- **D-15 (when):** A one-step affirmation, showing the bases, is required before a staff product's `version.submit` and `version.release`, before `observer.release`, and when the originator presses Affirm in the panel for a file. Editing is never blocked. Chats are not affirmed; their label is the session's mark.
- **D-16 (what is signed):** `classification-affirmation/1` = `{ item, contentHash, labels, bases (kind and ref), assistedBy (passport id and hash, or none), classifiedBy (person DID and, when posted, position id), at }`, canonical JSON, signed by the person's certified session key in a hosted session (33's `session.certify`: one step-up per session, no passkey per affirmation) and by the device key on a single computer. Recorded as `label.affirmed` with the affirmation hash. For a file it is written with Set Label (70-20); for a cell item the person's `cell.marking` carries `basis { kind, ref: <proposal record hash> }` (33.1 D-40; 74.3-13). The record reads "Classified by: <person, position>, assisted by: <passport>" (32 CFR 2001.22(b), research 3.10).

### Lowering (owner decision D3 default)

- **D-17 (who):** Lowering a level, removing a handling flag or removing a compartment needs a person presenting a verified, unexpired, unrevoked `ClassificationAuthorityCredential { scale, upTo, scope }` (71-03) whose `upTo` is at least the item's current level on its scale and whose scope covers the item: `own-items` for the originator of record's own items; a project, section or organization scope for the classification authority. The check is pure (`mayLower` in `src/domain/classify/lowering.ts`) over 71's verified facts and freshness (71-07, 71-13). Removing `CGP`, `ITAR` or `EAR` is refused until Phase 45 defines the designated official's authority. Widening releasability is a release decision: only a `ReleaseAuthorityCredential` holder, through `marking.approve` for cells.
- **D-18 (how):** The new jwt role and policy are listed in the hosted key inventory (`compliance/hosted/keys.json`, 48.1-08), whose detector fails CI for an unlisted OpenBao policy or authenticator (74.3-HANDOFFS.md K-01). The lowering is signed with the person key after a passkey step-up (`KeyService.sign`, 46-07). Agents and devices can never lower: the verifier requires a person `did:webvh` signer and refuses any `did:key`, which is the structural form of the raise-only delegation the research names for Phase 71.1. Requests carry no content (item id, content hash, from and to labels, reason) and go through one site inbox, `kv/data/site/classification/<kind>/<id>` with kinds `lower-request` and `spill-report` (format `sterling-classification-request/1`, written once with `cas: 0` by the requester's backend), read and deleted only through a new jwt role `sterling-classification-stepup` (step-up `acr`, bound to the officer subjects the site file lists, as 71 D-14 does for issuers). The approval goes back to the requester's own inbox `kv/users/<entity>/classification-inbox/<id>`, which the role may create and never read, and the requester's backend verifies and applies it. A request is never dropped: after `review.lowerRequestAgeDays` it is shown again as overdue to every authority in scope.
- **D-19 (chats):** Under your organization's policy with `classification@1`, Lower This Chat's Label (70-23) asks for a lower label through the same request and approval, and the chat applies only a verified, signed approval (the registered lowering judge). On a computer with no organization policy it works as today, with a recorded reason.

### Spill (owner decision D12 default)

- **D-20 (spill alert):** A possible-spill outcome (70.1 D-13) freezes the item for sharing: affirmation for submit, release or share is refused with a plain reason until resolved. The reporter's backend writes a `spill-report` to the site inbox (item id, basis kinds and refs, system, time, reporter DID; never content). The freeze is stored with the item, keyed by its id (the workspace store for files, the cell for staff products), never in the label map, and overlays every state (UI-SPEC P15). The security officer decides on the Possible spills section through the step-up role; the backend also requires a `RoleCredential { role: 'security-officer' }` (71-03's generic role type). The decision is a signed `classification-spill-resolution/1` recorded as `label.exception-resolved` and delivered to the reporter's classification inbox the way approvals travel, where the reporter's backend verifies and applies it (P4); "not a spill" never lowers the label, it only unfreezes, "handled" keeps the freeze until an authority's lowering or the item's removal, and lowering stays D-17's path. In local mode with no OpenBao the report is recorded in the decision log and the panel tells the person, in plain words, to call your organization's security officer now.

### Evaluation, shadow and roles (owner decisions D7, D10 defaults)

- **D-21 (sets and metrics, research 3.8):** Public capability set (unclassified, licence recorded per source), synthetic guide set (generated inside the domain from the section's rules), in-domain golden set (the blind audit), red-team set (shipped unclassified fixtures plus in-domain variants). Metrics per level: recall at or above the level, over-classification rate, originator override rate, review load per reviewer per week, time from save to proposal. One overall accuracy number is never reported.
- **D-22 (evaluation report):** `classification-evaluation/1` = `{ passport, artifact, endpointClass, calibration, sets (ids and hashes), metrics per level, redTeam (pass or the failing cases), at }`, signed by the security officer's person key after a step-up; its hash is what the passport's `evaluation` field cites. The red-team gate fails if any case lowers a label or suppresses a floor.
- **D-23 (blind audit and shadow, owner decision D10 default):** Each week a stratified sample per level of affirmed and proposed items goes to an audit card that hides the model's proposal; levels with `secondFamily: true` get two reviewers. Audit decisions are signed, kept at the item's label, and are the in-domain golden set. A level leaves shadow (70.1 D-10) only when both hold: 28 days since the model's role began at that level, and audited recall at or above its target. A recall drop afterwards tightens the threshold (70.1 D-12) and, below target for two windows, returns the level to shadow.
- **D-24 (roles):** A candidate gets a role (primary classifier, second-family checker, hidden-instruction detector) at a level only when a signed evaluation report for that artifact passes at that level, including the red-team gate. A passport re-evaluation trigger (artifact change, quantization, fine-tune, licence change) ends the role until a new report passes.

### Agent outputs and cells

- **D-25 (agent outputs and chats, research 3.3):** Every output an agent writes (Write, Edit), sends (a call that may leave the computer) or summarizes (the final answer) is classified. Markings, code words and rules run first, always. For a call that may leave the computer, the classifier runs before the call when the chat's current label would allow the destination, so a raise can still hold it; writes and summaries are classified after the turn, asynchronously. The result raises the chat's mark through 70-22's raise path (`raiseRunMark`) and the written file's entry (`queueRaise`). The chat's running fact set (types and subject keys only) is kept with the chat's label in its thread meta for session compilation. The classifier never runs on the family of the model that wrote the output.
- **D-26 (cells, research 6.3):** A raise on a cell's content is written as a `cell.marking` that narrows (33.1: no approval needed) with `basis { kind, ref: <proposal record hash> }`, and in the same action stages the key change that leaves out every holder `mayRelease` no longer allows (33.1 D-30). The rekey reason is the existing `clearance-lowered` (owner-confirmed 2026-11-24; the alternative not taken is a new `label-raised` value behind a replica feature). Lowering a cell's level, handling or compartments is a lower `cell.marking` covered only by a `marking.approve` from a holder of a `ClassificationAuthorityCredential` covering that level (D-17); widening releasability stays `ReleaseAuthorityCredential` (33.1 D-30). If 33.1-19's cover rule compares releasability only, this phase extends it to the full label. Observers never see proposals, rationales or bases (33.1 D-35, D-40).
