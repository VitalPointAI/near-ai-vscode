<objective>
Complete the storage contract: add the conversation binding and pending approval operations, and prove every aggregate's semantics with one reusable contract suite plus schema versioning tests.

Purpose: RELAY-03 names five kinds of state; D-04 puts them behind one interface; D-05 makes the contract tests the proof that a fallback store could replace `node:sqlite`. This is the invariant test accepted in plan 03's assumption-delta decision.
Output: binding and approval operations, `test/contract/relayStore.contract.ts`, `test/sqliteStore.test.ts`, `test/schema.test.ts`.

Recorded choices: approvals mirror the extension's approval shape (`src/agent/loop.ts` ApprovalRequest callId, tool, title; ApprovalReply decisions other than `pending`) so Phase 73 maps them one to one. Bindings and tenants are global aggregates; messages, runs and approvals are tenant-scoped (plan 03 assumption-delta decision). The JSON-file-per-tenant store itself stays deferred (CONTEXT Deferred Ideas).
</objective>
