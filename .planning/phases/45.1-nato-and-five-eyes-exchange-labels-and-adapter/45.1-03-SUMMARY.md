## What Task 3 changed

- `agreementsSection.ts`: an `agreements@1` item may carry `clearanceProcessVetted { by, on, basis }` (by at most 200 characters, basis at most 500, on a timestamp), read with the section's own `checkKeys`, `parseText` and `parseTimestamp` into a frozen object. A missing or extra key, empty text or a bad time is refused with the section's existing reasons. Without the field the agreement parses as before, not vetted. `layers.ts` is unchanged: `agreements.items` is already organization-only with its subtree, so only your organization's signed layer can mark a partner vetted, and a test pins that.
- `exportCheck.ts`: `ExportContext` gains an optional `recipient` of the new `ExportRecipient { did, facts, policy }` (policy required in the type). After the agreement's own `mayRelease` passes, a vetting that is whole and dated on or before now gives basis `vetted-partner`. Otherwise canExport requires, in order: a recipient (`partner-not-vetted`), its credentials section (`no-credential-policy`), checked facts whose subject is the recipient's DID (`recipient-mismatch`), a token among the agreement's tokens (`recipient-not-partner`), and `mayRelease` with that section, the scales, mappings and now (`recipient-not-cleared`, carrying `mayRelease`'s reason). It then passes with basis `recipient-credentials` and the recipient's DID on the decision. The doc comment now says the agreement stands for the partner's own vetting only when your organization has recorded it.
- `partner-not-vetted` is the owner's sentence word for word: "This partner's clearance process hasn't been vetted. Vet it first, or the recipient must provide their clearance details."
- The Task 2 fixture agreement now carries a vetting dated before NOW, so the Task 2 tests still test what they tested; no existing assertion changed.
