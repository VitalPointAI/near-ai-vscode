A person who joins a shared project late gets the same product text at once, marked unverified. Their computer then fetches and checks the earlier history in the background, and once it is in, has the same digest as everyone else. The one exception, changes made after the checkpoint in an editing session already open at it, stays open as G431 for 33-33 (D-71).

Purpose: 33-14 built the start from a trusted checkpoint, but the joiner's text waits for registrations written before that checkpoint, and nothing fetches the earlier history. D-70 does three things:
- It lets a compaction stand in for those registrations only where the roster and a trusted base vouch for it.
- It fetches the earlier history after going live, chain-checks it, and uses it to recompute the base. A server that lies about the base becomes a forked-view finding.
- It keeps the check from being skipped by two independent paths: the obligation is saved before the history it covers, and any gap in the held chain is fetched whatever the record says.
