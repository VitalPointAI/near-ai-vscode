# Administration and Standing Orders {#administration-and-organization-rules}

Open **SovAI: Standing Orders** from the Command Palette or select **Standing Orders** in the Workbench sidebar. Older security guides call the command **SovAI: Policy**. Their **Edit my rules**, **Apply to my rules** and **Reset my rules** buttons now read **Edit my Standing Orders**, **Apply to my Standing Orders** and **Reset my Standing Orders**.

This guide is coming with the guided setup. It will be written once the names of the setup steps
and settings are final, so that it matches what you see in the extension.

It will cover your organization's Standing Orders, including labels and clearances, model access,
spending limits, and decision records.

Until then, [Features and settings](../features.synced.md) describes every feature, and
[Getting started](../getting-started.md) gets you connected.
